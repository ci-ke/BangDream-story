event324-07 Ending: Could You Manage to Do Nothing?

The next morning, the group enjoys a light walk before breakfast. Just as it seems everything will end without incident...

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[The Next Morning]

Kaoru: Ah... The sunlight feels so refreshing. What a pleasant, fleeting morning...
Kokoro: And the clip-clop of our geta is so much fun!
Kanon: It really does make taking a walk more fun.
Hagumi: I wonder what's for breakfast~? The fish we had for dinner last night was soooo good, so I can't wait!
Misaki: I think they said breakfast is a buffet. They probably have both Japanese and Western food.
Kaoru: Steaming white rice and warm miso soup are hard to pass up... but freshly baked croissants and omelets sound delightful too. Ah... What a dilemma.
Misaki: I get it. I've actually been pretty excited for it too.
Kanon: Fufu, me too.
Kokoro: Hmm~...
Hagumi: What's wrong, Kokoron? Are you having trouble picking between rice and croissants too?
Kokoro: No, that's not it.
Kokoro: We did so many fun things, but in the end, I still don't really understand what doing nothing means.
Hagumi: Good point! Did we actually do nothing the right way?
Kanon: Hmm~... If there was any time we managed it, it might've been when we were dizzy from the hot spring...?
Misaki: Oh~... Yeah, I mean, that's true...
Misaki: But nothing wildly chaotic happened like it usually does, so for Hello, Happy World!, I'd say we did pretty well, right?
Hagumi: Really?
Misaki: Yeah, really. We did a good job doing nothing.
Hagumi: Yay~! We managed to do nothing! We did it, Kokoron!
Kokoro: Yes! We really did it!
Kaoru: Fufu... Kaoru Seta's horizons have broadened once more.
Misaki: Haha... I don't get what's got you so happy.
Misaki: But, well... I guess being this energetic is more Hello, Happy World!'s style.
Kanon: Yeah, seeing everyone like this makes me smile too.
Misaki: It does.
Hagumi: Huh? Is it just me, or is the ground around here giving off a ton of steam?
Kokoro: You're right. And it feels like it's shaking a little too. There must be a big mole hiding underneath!
Kaoru: What a passionate mole! Steam rises one burst after another, like its heated breath!
Kanon: Steam...?!
Kokoro: Look. It's right under our feet!
Misaki: Wait, could that be...?
Misaki: Everyone, get away from here!
Hagumi: Huh? But...
Misaki: Just hurry!!
Hagumi: Whoa?! Amazing! Hot water's shooting out of the ground!
Kokoro: Incredible! Is this a hot spring attraction?!
Kaoru: Ah... A surprise prepared by nature, you might say. What a magnificent entertainer.
Misaki & Kanon: ...
Misaki: ...No way...
Kanon: ...A-A hot spring just... appeared...
Misaki: Kanon-san... Does this mean we just discovered a new hot spring...?
Kanon: I think so~...
Hagumi: What?! Then does that make it the Hello, Happy World! Hot Spring?! Yay~!!
Kokoro: Wonderful! Then let's build a Hello, Happy World! hot spring inn too!
Hagumi: Wow! That's the best idea ever, Kokoron!
Kaoru: Ah, what a splendid idea. We could even put on Hello, Happy World! community theater in the rest area. Fufu, my heart is dancing already.
Kokoro: Then let's decide what kind of hot spring Hello, Happy World!'s hot spring should be!
Kokoro, Hagumi & Kaoru: Yeah~!!
Misaki: Haha... This was supposed to be a hot spring trip where we didn't do anything, but right at the very end...
Misaki: Seriously, why does Hello, Happy World! always, always...
Kanon: Ahaha... But maybe this is what makes us Hello, Happy World!
Misaki: Maybe... Ahaha...
Misaki: Sigh~~~~~~~~~...
(Black cut)
