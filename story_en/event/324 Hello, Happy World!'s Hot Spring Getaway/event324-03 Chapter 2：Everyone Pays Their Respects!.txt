event324-03 Chapter 2: Everyone Pays Their Respects!

After enjoying some sightseeing along the way, the group arrives at the shrine. Each of them seems to have their own wish...

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[Shrine]

Hagumi: We made it~! We're at the top~!
Misaki: Phew... There were a lot of stairs, huh?
Kanon: But since we're so deep in the mountains, the air feels really clean and refreshing.
Kaoru: Indeed. Even from the shrine grounds, you can sense a quiet, sacred atmosphere.
Misaki: You can say that again... The rustling trees and chirping birds... It's kinda soothing...
Kokoro: Misaki! Let's put money in the offering box!
Hagumi: Hagumi wants to ring the bell! Huh? Do you shake the rope before you put in your offering, or after?
Kanon: I think you ring the bell before the offering.
Misaki: Just so we're clear, you can't be too noisy in places like this, okay?
Kokoro, Hagumi & Kaoru: Okaaay!
Misaki: Let's see, my offering, my offering...
Hagumi: Hey, hey, has everyone decided what to wish for?
Kokoro: I'm wishing for the whole world to smile, of course!
Kanon: That's so like you, Kokoro-chan.
Misaki: Ahaha... It sure is. Though I'm not sure if that's a wish or a goal.
Kaoru: I'm planning to pray about my future. I want to keep growing as an actor.
Misaki: Wow. That's so you, Kaoru-san.
Hagumi: I'm gonna wish to get into college~!
Kanon: You've been super curious about college lately, Hagumi-chan.
Hagumi: Yeah! I still don't really know much about it, but if I'm gonna go, I feel like I need to get better at studying!
Hagumi: Oh, I know! Then I'll wish to get good scores on my next test!
Misaki: Hagumi, thinking about schoolwork... You've really grown up...
Hagumi: I'm a third-year now, you know!
Misaki: (Right... Hagumi's seriously thinking about her future in her own way.)
Kokoro: Okay, is everyone ready?
Kokoro, Hagumi & Kaoru: ...
Misaki: (Even the three dummies get quiet at times like this. Maybe they're taking their prayers seriously...)
Misaki: (...Then I guess I'll pray for-)
(Black cut)
Misaki: Okay. Now that we're done praying, why don't we head to the inn?
Kokoro: Yes! Let's go!
Kaoru: Apparently, the open-air bath has a stunning ocean view. I'm very much looking forward to it.
Hagumi: Me too! I can't wait for the free-flowing hot spring!
Misaki: Good grief. Are we really gonna be able to relax at the inn with them acting like this~...?
Kanon: Fufu, I'm sure it'll be fine.
Kanon: Misaki-chan, you were praying for a while back there. What did you wish for?
Misaki: Huh? I didn't realize I took that long. That's kinda embarrassing...
Kanon: It's not embarrassing at all. It just means you had a really important wish.
Misaki: Well, actually-
Hagumi: Mii-kun! Kano-chan-senpai! They have original Japanese yam croquettes!
Kaoru: Ah, that rich aroma... How very appetizing...
Misaki: Geez~ You guys just had hot spring manju. If you eat that much, you won't have room for dinner at the inn.
Kokoro: But our walk along the mountain path made me hungry!
Misaki: I can't tell if you burn through fuel fast or slow.
Kanon: Fufu, why not? Eating is one of the best parts of traveling.
Misaki: True enough. Guess we might as well enjoy that part too.
Hagumi: Mii-kun! Kano-chan-senpai! They said it's fresh out of the fryer~~! Hurry, hurry~!
Misaki: Okay~! Coming~!
