event324-01 Opening: I Want to Go to a Hot Spring

In the middle of a lively Hello, Happy World! meeting. Misaki seems to be somewhere else in her mind...

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[Tsurumaki Residence]

Hagumi: And so, since a new shop is opening downtown, they want us to help hand out flyers.
Kokoro: Got it! Let's all have a flyer parade together!
Kaoru: Fufu, my skills are tingling. Let's make it so lively, customers come pouring in from far and wide.
Kokoro: Then it's decided! The week after next will be our flyer parade!
Hagumi, Kaoru & Kanon: Yeah!
Kokoro: Now, our next topic is... Misaki, what was it again?
Misaki: ...
Kanon: Misaki-chan?
Misaki: I wanna go to a hot spring...
Hagumi: Huh?
Kanon: A hot spring?
Misaki: ...Oh! Sorry, sorry. I kinda spaced out for a second.
Hagumi: Mii-kun, did your bathtub at home break? Want Hagumi to help fix it?
Kaoru: Not being able to take a nice, relaxing bath is a serious matter. I'll lend what little strength I have as well.
Kokoro: Yes! Time to send out the Hello, Happy World! carpentry team!
Misaki: No, no, that's not it. Our bath at home is fine.
Hagumi: Really? Then why hot springs?
Misaki: Oh, uh~... Someone on the student council went on a hot spring trip with their family. After hearing about it, I started wanting to go too.
Misaki: I heard the scenery was gorgeous, and the ryokan was super cozy, so I thought it'd be nice to relax somewhere like that~.
Kanon: A hot spring trip, huh? Now I wanna go on one too. It's been ages.
Kokoro: Then let's all go together! There's a hot spring we discovered recently across the ocean!
Hagumi: Wow, that sounds fun!
Hagumi: If it's a hot spring owned by Kokoron's family, it's gotta have amusement park-style attractions too!
Kokoro: Yes, it's a spa resort surrounded by tropical rainforest! We can go adventuring out in the great outdoors!
Kaoru: An exotic spa resort... How thrilling.
Misaki: No, that's not what I mean... I want a more normal hot spring trip.
Kokoro, Hagumi & Kaoru: A normal hot spring trip?
Hagumi: What's normal supposed to mean?
Misaki: Uh... How do I put it~...? Like relaxing in a Japanese-style room at a ryokan?
Kanon: I think I get what Misaki-chan means. Taking a nice, relaxing walk through a hot spring district, eating all the yummy local food...
Kanon: It's less about doing something and more like choosing not to do anything, right?
Misaki: Yes, exactly! That's it, Kanon-san!
Misaki: I knew you'd understand.
Kanon: Fufu, you're making it sound like a bigger deal than it is.
Kokoro: Not doing anything... I'm not totally sure what that means, but it sounds fun!
Hagumi: Yeah, yeah! If we're with everyone in Hello, Happy World!, any trip will be a blast!
Kaoru: Indeed. Wherever we are, as long as everyone's there, it becomes the ultimate paradise.
Kokoro: Then this weekend, let's go do "nothing"!
Hagumi: Mii-kun, is there any hot spring you wanna go to?
Misaki: Huh? Not really... Maybe somewhere quiet where we can just relax?
Kokoro: Quiet and relaxing! Got it!
Kaoru: I looked it up on my phone, and this hot spring seems to have a great reputation for its benefits.
Hagumi: Let's see... "Gensen kakenagashi"... Wow, that sounds kinda strong!
Kokoro: How wonderful! Misaki, what do you think of this hot spring?
Misaki: Sure, but... Huh? Aren't things moving a little too smoothly?
Kanon: Fufu, before we knew it, we had a hot springs trip planned.
Misaki: Well, that's nothing new... Kanon-san, are you okay with college and everything?
Kanon: Yeah. I'm done with my assignments, and I don't have any plans, so I'll be fine.
Misaki: I see... Well, I don't have student council or work either...
Kanon: Then that's perfect. This is a good chance, so let's take it easy together for once.
Misaki: You're right. Then... should we go? To the hot springs?
