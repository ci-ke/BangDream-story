event324-06 Chapter 5: A Peaceful Moment

Misaki wakes up from her nap. It seems to have already gotten quite dark outside...

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

Misaki: ...Mm... What time is it... ?
Misaki: Whoa, it's pitch-black outside... Oh, but there's still some time before dinner...
Kanon: Misaki-chan, are you awake?
Misaki: Oh, you were up, Kanon-san.
Kanon: Yeah. There's some cold water over here. Want some?
Misaki: Thank you. I think I will.
Misaki: Gulp, gulp... Ahh~... Aah~... water tastes so good~.
Kanon: We did sweat a lot in the hot spring.
Misaki: Sigh~... This space is so relaxing~...
Kanon: Yeah. I really like these kinds of areas at ryokan.
Misaki: You too, Kanon-san? Same here. Um, what were these spots called again?
Kanon: I think... it's called a hiroen.
Misaki: Oh, that's it. It's the perfect amount of cramped, and you can just relax and gaze out at the scenery.
Kanon: Yeah...
Misaki & Kanon: ...
Misaki: It's so quiet...
Kanon: It's like time's moving slower here. It was so lively just a little while ago, too.
Misaki: Yeah.
Misaki: I thought we'd be able to relax in the hot springs, but things got pretty noisy, one way or another...
Kokoro: ...Zzz... Zzz...
Hagumi: Mm... Zzz... Zzz...
Misaki: ...But... it almost feels a little too quiet now.
Kanon: ...Yeah, I get that.
Kanon: Oh, right. I never got to ask, but what did you wish for at the shrine?
Misaki: Oh~, that was, uh... How do I put it...? Stuff about my future, I guess.
Kanon: I see.
Misaki: Yeah. I don't have anything specific in mind yet, like what I want to be. I just hope I'll figure it out someday.
Misaki: Oh, but it's not like I'm worried about the future or anything, okay?
Misaki: It's more like... I want to study hard in my own way, get into a good college, and keep my options open.
Misaki: I want to do what I can now so my future self can become anything.
Kanon: So you can be anything you want...?
Misaki: Yeah... Back when I first started high school, I didn't really think that way.
Misaki: I figured I'd study enough to get by, go to a decent college, and find a stable job... Something like that.
Misaki: And of course, I don't think there's anything wrong with that. ...But now, I feel like there might be a lot more paths open to me than I realized.
Misaki: As long as I don't tell myself I can't do something, I can probably become anything I want... That's how it feels.
Misaki: Ahaha... Sorry. I kinda started rambling out of nowhere.
Kanon: No. ...You've changed, Misaki-chan.
Kanon: Your eyes are sparkling right now.
Misaki: No, no, it's not anything that impressive. It's just how I feel at the moment. I might end up deciding a stable job sounds better after all.
Kokoro: ...Zzz...
Misaki: Well, maybe the people around me have influenced me a little.
Kanon: Fufu... True. Just a little.
Misaki: ...Something about the way you said that is bugging me.
Kanon: Huh~? I didn't mean anything by it~.
Hagumi: Mm... Mii-kun, Kano-chan-senpai...? Yaaawn... Morning~...
Misaki: Oh. Hagumi, you're awake?
Hagumi: Huh~? When did I fall asleep...?
Kanon: Hagumi-chan, do you wanna come over here and drink some water?
Hagumi: Yeah.
Misaki: It's about time. Why don't we go get dinner?
Misaki: Kokoro, Kaoru-san... Wake up. It's time to eat~.
