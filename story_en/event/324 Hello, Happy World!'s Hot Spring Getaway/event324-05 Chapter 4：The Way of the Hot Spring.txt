event324-05 Chapter 4: The Way of the Hot Spring

The group wastes no time enjoying the hot spring they came for. Idle chatter blooms between them...

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[Hot Spring]

Kokoro: Mmm~~, what a wonderful view! And it's so spacious. Hagumi, let's race using the front crawl!
Hagumi: Kokoron, non, non, non! You're not supposed to swim in hot springs!
Kokoro: Oh, really?
Hagumi: When Hagumi was little, she went to a hot spring with her family and started doing the front crawl, and Mom got super mad at her~.
Hagumi: She said, "You're splashing everyone around you!" Hot springs are supposed to be for everyone to relax and enjoy together!
Misaki: Haha... I can picture exactly how Hagumi's mom felt back then.
Hagumi: So when you're in a hot spring, relaxing and taking it easy is best.
Kokoro: I see. Whenever I've gone to a spa before, I would swim as much as I wanted.
Misaki: Well, the places you've gone to were probably private ones owned by the Tsurumaki family... As long as you don't swim in hot springs, you're fine.
Kaoru: Soaking in the water like this and enjoying a lovely conversation... That's the real charm of a hot spring.
Kanon: Yeah. I think that's what "doing nothing" means on a hot spring trip.
Kokoro: So this is "doing nothing"... All right, I'll try staying still.
Hagumi: Kokoron, you should make sure you're soaked up to your shoulders so you don't get cold!
Kokoro: Like this?
Hagumi: Yeah, yeah! Just like that!
Kokoro: My whole body is starting to feel nice and warm... I wish Michelle could've come too...
Misaki: Ah~... Well, Michelle's a bear, so hot springs are kind of tough for her.
Kanon: She said she was looking forward to hearing all about it, so let's make sure we tell her everything.
Kaoru: Then, as we soak, shall we partake in some elegant conversation? Speaking of hot springs, when I was five years old-
(Black cut)
Misaki: Sigh~... It feels like my whole body and mind are melting away...
Kanon: Fufu. You look so relaxed, Misaki-chan. Have studying and your student council president duties been wearing you out?
Misaki: Maybe so~...
Misaki: Of course, Ichigaya-san and the others have been helping me out a ton, but I guess I've still been kind of tense...
Kanon: It's hard to notice stuff like that in your normal everyday life. Maybe you only realized because this trip is so laid-back.
Misaki: Yeah~... Whenever Hello, Happy World! goes somewhere, things tend to get pretty over-the-top... So this kind of trip is really rare for us.
Kanon: Fufu. But that's what makes Hello, Happy World! who we are.
Misaki: That's why this feels so meaningful... It's so nice and quiet...
Kanon: It is. I don't think I've had time lately to just clear my mind and listen to the sounds of nature...
Misaki: ...the sound of the waves, the wind...
Kanon: Birds calling in the distance...
Misaki & Kanon: ...
Misaki: ...Wait, isn't it a little too quiet? The three dummies are all here, but it's this peaceful...!
Kanon: Y-you're right...! Kaoru-san? Is everything okay over there~?
Kaoru: Yes. I was just emptying my mind and conversing with the water.
Misaki: I-I see...
Kokoro: Hey, Misaki~... Am I doing the whole doing-nothing thing right?
Hagumi: Is Hagumi doing it right too~?
Misaki: Uh, yeah. I think so, but...
Misaki: Don't all three of you seem kind of off? Your voices sound all melty...
Kokoro: I'm the same as always~...
Hagumi: Hagumi too~...
Kaoru: As am I...
Kanon: Wait, maybe Kokoro-chan and the others are overheated from the bath...!
Misaki: Whoa! You're right, their faces are bright red! Everybody, get out, now!
Kokoro: Huh~...? But it feels so nice~...
Hagumi: Hagumi wants to soak longer~...
Misaki: No more! Come on, stand up slowly... It's dangerous to get out too quickly!
Kanon: I-I'll go get some cold water~!
(Black cut)
(Background change)
Kokoro, Hagumi & Kaoru: ...Zzz... Zzz...
Misaki: Phew... They're sleeping quietly in their futons, so they should be okay for now, right?
Kanon: There's still some time before dinner... I'm sure they'll be feeling better by then.
Misaki: Man, I was worried about how that would turn out... They were red as boiled octopuses...
Kanon: I'm glad we noticed before they got really overheated.
Misaki: You really can't take your eyes off Kokoro and the others for a second.
Kokoro: ...Mmh... Misaki...
Misaki: Honestly... Sleeping away without knowing how worried they made us. ...Yaaawn~.
Kanon: Yaaawn... Fufu, I guess your yawn rubbed off on me, Misaki-chan.
Misaki: Ahaha, sorry about that.
Kanon: I'm starting to feel kind of sleepy too.
Misaki: The hot spring felt so nice... Maybe we should get some sleep too.
Kanon: Yeah, let's do that.
Misaki: I'll set a timer for dinner... Okay, that should do it.
Kanon: Thanks, Misaki-chan. Okay, let's rest for a bit. Good night.
Misaki: Good night... Kanon-san.
(Black cut)
(Background change)
