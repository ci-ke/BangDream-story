event316-04 Chapter 3: Prince of the Fleeting Realm

The script for the show's main event is done. But who has Kaoru cast as the princess...?

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[Tsurumaki Residence]

Kokoro: Kaoru, is it true the play's script is finished?!
Kaoru: Yes. At long last, my finest fleeting tale is complete.
Kaoru: Now, here it is. Everyone, take a copy.
Misaki: U-um...? The title is "The Magical Prince of the Fleeting Kingdom"...?
Kaoru: It's a romance between a prince and a princess, from rival countries, in a world where magic exists.
Kaoru: The two meet by chance and fall in love, and after overcoming all sorts of obstacles, the princess finally finds her freedom and is united with the prince... A fleeting story like that.
Hagumi: Whoa, that's awesome, awesome! I'm so excited! So, what roles are we playing?!
Kaoru: Fufu, don't rush so much. I'll explain in order.
Kaoru: First, the prince will be played by me, Kaoru Seta. Michelle will play the princess's mother, who tries to keep the prince and princess apart.
Kaoru: Kanon and Kokoro will play castle servants who cheer on the princess's romance. And Hagumi will play the princess who falls in love with the prince.
Hagumi: Huh?! I'm the princess?!
Kokoro: How lovely! It's perfect for you!
Kanon: Yeah. I can't wait to see Princess Hagumi-chan.
Hagumi: A princess... I wonder if I can do it...?
Kaoru: This is a role I can only ask you to play, Hagumi.
Hagumi: B-but...
Kaoru: Of course, I won't force you into it... But if I can, I'd really like you to do it.
Kaoru: So... what do you think...?
Hagumi: A role only I can play...
Hagumi: Yeah, okay! If that's the case, I'll do my best!
Kaoru: Thank you, Hagumi. I knew you'd say that.
Kokoro: All right, then let's read through the script and try it together!
Misaki: Can you all head over to the garden first? I'm gonna go give the script to Michelle.
Kaoru: Yeah, thanks, Misaki.
Kaoru: All right, let's go. Let's make this the greatest show to headline Kaoru Seta Land...!
(Black cut)
(Background change)
Michelle: "Honestly, that prince... He's been nothing but trouble...!"
Michelle: "But now he won't lay a finger on my daughter. I asked the strongest witch in the land to lock the princess away at the top of a tower!"
Michelle: "She can spend the rest of her life in that room, like a caged bird! Hee~ hee hee hee!"
Kokoro: "Princess, we'll definitely come save you!"
Kanon: "But... maybe that prince will save the princess..."
Hagumi: U-um... "Ah, so... was I wrong to seek freedom after all...?"
Hagumi: "If it was going to make me feel like this... then I wish I never knew him, um... or that happiness..."
Kokoro: Hagumi, I think if you speak up more, it'll reach everyone in the audience!
Hagumi: S-sorry. Let's start over from the beginning!
Kanon: Hagumi-chan, why don't you take a short break? This scene seems pretty tough.
Michelle: Yeah, yeah. Don't push yourself too hard~
Hagumi: I-I'm fine! This is a play Kaoru-kun's fans have been looking forward to...
Hagumi: Hagumi wants to be able to do it right!
Kaoru: Hagumi...
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[Tsurumaki Residence - Hallway]

Hagumi: Sigh...
Kaoru: Hey there, Hagumi.
Hagumi: Kaoru-kun...
Kaoru: It's break time, but... what's wrong? Why are you off by yourself?
Kaoru: Here, I've got some fruit for you. I brought the kind you like, Hagumi. Want some?
Hagumi: Yeah, thanks... Nom. Ehehe, it's sweet and tangy... So good...
Kaoru: Feels like even the air tastes better here. I think I'll take a break here too. Mind if I sit next to you?
Hagumi: Yeah...
Hagumi: ...Sorry, Kaoru-kun. I don't think I can be a princess after all.
Hagumi: I'm not really graceful like a princess, and the last scene is kind of embarrassing... No matter how many times I try, I can't get it right...
Kaoru: Hmm...
Kaoru: Hagumi, this is only my imagination... but could you listen for a moment?
Hagumi: Yeah...
Kaoru: I think you're more delicate and charming than you realize.
Kaoru: And not just me. I'm sure Kokoro and Misaki, Kanon, and Michelle think so too.
Hagumi: Really...?
Kaoru: Yes. So... you're fine just the way you are.
Kaoru: Let the lines melt into you, and just be there. Don't force it, and don't overthink it. If you do, I'm sure you'll become a princess that's true to you, Hagumi.
Hagumi: I see...
Hagumi: But... what if I still can't do it when it really counts...?!
Kaoru: You'll be okay, Hagumi.
Kaoru: If that happens, then I, the prince, will make sure you become a princess. So don't worry.
Hagumi: ...Yeah, got it!
Hagumi: Kaoru-kun, you picked this because you thought it was perfect for Hagumi, right? Then I'll trust how you feel and give it my best shot!
Hagumi: And moping around any longer isn't like Hagumi anyway... Let's just practice! One more time!
Kaoru: Ah, you finally smiled. That sparkling smile suits you so well.
Kaoru: Now, let's go. To enjoy the audience... And above all, to enjoy the stage.
