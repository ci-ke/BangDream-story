event316-07 Ending: Kaoru Seta Land Forever

Kaoru Seta Land ends in a roaring success. Talk at the after-party turns, naturally, to the show-

(Character: Kokoro Tsurumaki, Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Kanon Matsubara, Misaki Okusawa)

[Tsurumaki Residence]

Kokoro: All right, everyone~...
All: Cheers~!
Misaki: Man... We managed to close down Seta Kaoru Land without any problems.
Kanon: Kaoru-san, great job again. You had to perform in tons of events, so it must've been tough, right?
Kaoru: Not at all. I got to see so many fleeting smiles... If anything, my heart is filled with happiness.
Kaoru: Everyone, once again... Thank you so much for all the hard work you put into opening Seta Kaoru Land.
Kaoru: Thanks to you, I was able to grant so many of my little kittens' fleeting, beautiful wishes.
Misaki: No, no. We had fun too, in our own way.
Misaki: And we made some new discoveries. It really hit me again just how amazing you are, Kaoru-san.
Kanon: Yeah. Plus, seeing how happy Kaoru-san's fans were up close made me happy too.
Misaki: Definitely. And we got to see Hagumi in that adorable princess outfit.
Kokoro: Yes! Even now, just thinking back on it... She was the cutest princess ever!
Kanon: That last scene totally made my heart skip a beat.
Hagumi: Ehehe, thanks!
Hagumi: When I tripped onstage, I panicked like, "What do I do?!" but...
Hagumi: Then afterward, I was surprised I could talk so smoothly~ Even I couldn't believe it.
Misaki: Yeah, yeah. It was like you were completely in character... I got totally pulled in watching you.
Hagumi: It was kind of weird. It didn't feel like me, but it was like I was the one talking...
Hagumi: It felt like I met a new version of myself, and it was exciting... My heart was pounding, too!
Hagumi: So I'm really glad I did it! Thanks for making me the princess, Kaoru-kun!
Kaoru: Fufu... If you felt even a little of what makes theater so special, I couldn't be happier.
Kaoru: And delivering the very best smiles to all my little kittens... Of course, that includes you too, Hagumi.
Hagumi: Kaoru-kun...!
Misaki: Oh, look. Even in the attendee surveys, people wrote that Princess Hagumi was cute. They said, "I want to see it again."
Kokoro: My! So there were surveys, too! Let's read them together!
Kanon: There's one over here, too. "Hagumi-chan and Kaoru-san as the princess and prince were so wonderful."
Hagumi: Ehehe, that's kind of embarrassing~ But I'm so happy!
Kokoro: This one's about the greeting, too! "Thank you for the wonderful time. If you hold it again, I'll definitely come!"
Kanon: This one says, "I'm sorry I cried back then. I'll never forget the warmth of Kaoru-san's hand."
Misaki: This one's feedback about the exhibits.
Misaki: "I got to fully appreciate Kaoru-san's fleeting, profound side that I never knew about, and it made me so happy. Next time you hold this event, I'll bring my friends who couldn't come today," it says.
Misaki: Fufu, reading stuff like this just makes me happy.
Kaoru: I'm truly grateful. I'll start writing replies to each one right away.
Misaki: You're writing back to the visitor surveys, too?!
Kaoru: To meet my fans' expectations... No, to deliver a moving experience that exceeds them. That's Seta Kaoru's mission, and what brings me happiness.
Misaki: Whoa... That's so Kaoru-san...
Hagumi: There are so many messages in the surveys... Everyone's really looking forward to the next Seta Kaoru Land!
Kanon: Yeah. Every message says they're excited for the next one.
Misaki: Yeah. That means everyone had a lot of fun, and as fans, it makes sense they'd start looking forward to what's next.
Misaki: ...Oh, which means...
Kaoru: Fufu, don't worry.
Kaoru: Of course, I've already started drafting the concept for the second one.
Kaoru: Next time, it'll be even bigger, and it'll make even more of my little kittens smile... A dreamland created by Seta Kaoru...
Kaoru: No, a dream world... Yes, I'll build Seta Kaoru World.
Kokoro: Amazing, Kaoru! You're gonna create a whole world!
Hagumi: Yay~! Hey, hey, what're we doing next, Kaoru-kun?!
Kaoru: Let's see... First, we'll screen a film about Kaoru Seta's life story in a dome, and then-
Kanon: Kaoru-san... You really do care about your fans.
Misaki: Yeah... Maybe it's because you're so devoted to them that everyone's totally hooked on you...
Kaoru: Fufu, just wait for it, my little kittens.
Kaoru: I'll show you a fleeting moment that surpasses even the hopes you hold in your hearts-!
