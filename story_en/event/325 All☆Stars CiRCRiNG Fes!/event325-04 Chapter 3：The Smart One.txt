event325-04 Chapter 3: The Smart One

With Tutu as the conductor, practice continues with high energy and precision.

(Character: Saya Yamabuki, Aya Maruyama, Chisato Shirasagi, Sayo Hikawa, Chiyu Tamade)

[CHU²'s Penthouse - Studio]

CHU² (Chiyu): Okay! That's enough. That was quite good.
CHU² (Chiyu): But, Saya. It's painfully obvious you're holding back. Play like you usually do and go all out.
Saya: Sorry, I got a little too careful.
Chisato: Fufu, I understand how Saya-chan feels. I was also worried about how everyone else was doing.
Sayo: It's hard not to be extra conscious of listening to everyone's sound more than usual.
Aya: Yeah~. Besides, CHU²-san only said that because she recognizes how good Saya-chan is, right?
Saya: Then I need to work even harder.
CHU² (Chiyu): D-don't put words in my mouth! More importantly, Aya! You were tending to rush the tempo.
Aya: Huh, really?!
CHU² (Chiyu): Your singing has a brightness and flair that draws people in. But getting ahead of the beat is nonsense.
Aya: Ugh, I'll be careful...
CHU² (Chiyu): Chisato, Sayo. Your playing is accurate, but make it more dynamic. Pay special attention to building up before the chorus.
Chisato: Yes, understood.
Sayo: Then I'll try emphasizing the accents from the B section.
CHU² (Chiyu): I'm glad you catch on quickly. Now then, we're running it again from the top!
Saya: All right, this time I'm really gonna go for it... Looking forward to playing with you all!
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[CHU²'s Penthouse - Living Room]

Aya: Phew~, great work, everyone~.
Saya: We're all totally drenched in sweat now.
Chisato: But thanks to that, our playing is starting to feel more unified.
Sayo: That's thanks to CHU²-san's precise direction.
Aya: Yeah, totally. Getting advice from a different perspective than our lesson teacher's has been a huge help.
CHU² (Chiyu): It's nothing special.
Saya: Um... Is anyone hungry? I actually brought some buns for everyone.
Aya: Wow, buns from your family's bakery, Saya-chan! Melon buns, red bean buns, and chocolate cornets too! They look so good~!
Chisato: Aya-chan, I'm sure you know this already, but be careful not to eat too much.
Aya: I-I know~! I'm just refueling after working so hard at practice!
Saya: CHU², I made a jerky sandwich for you too.
CHU² (Chiyu): H-hmph. Well, I suppose I don't mind taking one.
Saya: Fufu, go right ahead.
Aya: Mmm~, so good~. Oh, right. Is it okay if we have our next practice at CHU²-san's place too?
CHU² (Chiyu): Go ahead. Booking a studio would be a hassle, wouldn't it?
Chisato: Being able to practice in such an amazing studio feels almost luxurious.
Saya: Yeah, it's got so much equipment. I was really surprised the first time I came here.
Sayo: Since going pro, I've had the chance to use all kinds of studios, but you don't often see facilities this particular.
Aya: Thanks for letting us use it.
CHU² (Chiyu): It's not something you need to thank me for. You'd rather be able to give practice your all too, wouldn't you?
Chisato: Yes. Even if this is only a one-time band, if we're doing it, I want to make something good.
CHU² (Chiyu): I'm not going to slack off just because it's for a festival either. Music is only worth doing if you take it seriously.
Sayo: Of course.
Aya: Yeah! Let's give it everything we've got!
Saya: Hehe... I'm glad I'm in the same band as all of you.
Aya: Huh?
Saya: To be honest, when the lottery decided our members, I was a little nervous.
Chisato: Nervous?
Saya: I mean, Roselia and Pastel＊Palettes are pros, and RAS has played at lots of famous festivals... I'm just a regular high school student.
CHU² (Chiyu): You don't need to worry about that. You're a skilled drummer.
Saya: Thanks, CHU². Today's practice really made me realize something.
Saya: It doesn't matter if you're a pro or just a regular high school student. We all feel the same way about facing music head-on.
Saya: That's why I'm glad I ended up with this band.
Chisato: Fufu, I'm glad you're in our band too, Saya-chan.
Sayo: I feel the same way. More than anything, I'm grateful for how easy it is to communicate with everyone.
CHU² (Chiyu): Yes, it helps when people catch on quickly. And when they give you ten in return after you say one.
Aya: Maybe we actually have really good chemistry?!
Sayo: Perhaps we do. At the very least, I feel I would've had a much harder time in another band.
Chisato: Come to think of it, Kokoro-chan's band has some very unique... Or rather, energetic members.
CHU² (Chiyu): Ah, MASKING's band. It goes without saying, but she's quite the opinionated drummer, and the other members are also...
Aya: Don't worry! I'm sure they're getting along just fine, like us!
Chisato: Yes, I'm sure they are. Rather than worrying about other bands, let's focus on our own first.
Saya, Sayo, Aya & CHU²: Yes! Yeah. Right.
(Black cut)
(Background change)
