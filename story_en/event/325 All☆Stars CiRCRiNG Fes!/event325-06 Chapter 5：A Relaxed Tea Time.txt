event325-06 Chapter 5: A Relaxed Tea Time

A reward awaits after practice♪ Himari and the others go looking for a cafe, but...

(Character: Tae Hanazono, Himari Uehara, Kanon Matsubara, Hina Hikawa, Mashiro Kurata)

[Shopping District]

Hina: Mmm~~, I'm exhausted~!
Tae: We did play a ton of guitar today. I kinda want something sweet.
Himari: Then let's reward ourselves for practicing and go to a café! Our tired bodies need a sugar boost!
Hina & Tae: Agreed~!
Kanon: Fufu, I'm in too. What do you think, Mashiro-chan?
Mashiro: Y-yes...! I'd like to have some tea too...
Hina: Oh, but places might be pretty crowded at this time.
Himari: True... Hazawa Coffee's probably packed too~... Kanon-san, do you know any good places?
Kanon: Hmm, around here... I can't think of anywhere that's likely to have seats right now.
Himari: Yeah, I figured...
Hina: What a pickle~.
Mashiro: U-um...!
Kanon: Mashiro-chan?
Mashiro: I... might know a good place...
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[Café]

Himari: Wow~, what a lovely atmosphere!
Kanon: Looks like we can take it easy here. Thanks for showing us this place, Mashiro-chan.
Mashiro: O-oh, no... I've just been here a few times with the rest of Morfonica... I'm glad you like it.
Tae: So it's your regular spot, Mashiro. Like, "Hey, master, the usual."
Mashiro: Huh...?! I-I don't do that...
Hina: There are so many things on the menu~! Hey, hey~, what does our regular Mashiro-chan recommend?
Mashiro: I-I'm not really a regular~...
Himari: Come on, you two! You're making Mashiro-chan uncomfortable!
Hina: Ahaha, sorry, sorry. It's just so fun watching your expression change every few seconds, Mashiro-chan.
Kanon: But I wanna know what you recommend too, Mashiro-chan. What was good when you came here before?
Mashiro: Um... The cake made with seasonal fruit was really good... It had lots of fruit on top, and the cream was so sweet...
Tae: That sounds good~. It's decided. I'll have that.
Himari: Me too, me too!
Mashiro: Oh, then I'll get that too...
Kanon: Has everyone decided? Excuse me~! We'd like to order~!
(Black cut)

[A Few Minutes Later]

Himari: Mmm, the sponge is so fluffy~! And the cream is amazing! Mashiro-chan, this cake is super good!
Mashiro: Really? I'm so glad...
Kanon: The tea's delicious too. It's such a relaxing flavor.
Himari: I know what you mean~. Practice was intense today, so this is exactly what we needed.
Hina: We're in the final stretch, after all. I can't wait for the show♪
Tae: Same here. This band is really great.
Kanon: Fufu, I was a little worried at first, though.
Himari: On the first day, O-Tae and Hina-senpai weren't playing from the sheet music at all, so I had no idea how this would turn out.
Tae: That day, Hina-san's guitar sounded so cool... It gave me chills.
Hina: Yeah, yeah♪ O-Tae-chan really got into it, so I started having a ton of fun too!
Kanon: Halfway through, it was basically a jam session.
Mashiro: They told me to just sing however it felt boppin'♪ to me... Everything went dark for a second...
Hina: Well, thanks to that, the quality went way up, so it all worked out in the end~.
Mashiro: I guess that's true, but~...
Himari: But Kanon-san, you stayed calm the whole time. You handled every bit of improv, and it was so cool!
Kanon: I-it really wasn't~...!
Kanon: I guess I'm just used to it. Things not going as planned, having to keep going with improv... That happens a lot in Hello, Happy World!
Himari: That's what makes you so cool~! I'm so glad you're in this band, Kanon-san!
Kanon: Thanks. But you've been a big help too, Himari-chan. You're always so cheerful and keep practice moving.
Tae: Yeah. You really made me think, wow, Himari's Afterglow's leader~.
Himari: Really~? Ehehe, I see~.
Tae: And Mashiro's singing is really nice for this band.
Mashiro: Huh?! M-me...?
Tae: Mashiro's vocals change into all kinds of colors to match the emotions, but every single one is so clear and beautiful. It makes playing guitar fun too.
Mashiro: I-I can't believe you'd say that about me...
Mashiro: I'm so glad I'm in this band...!
Kanon: I'm glad I got to play with all of you too. With this lineup, I'm sure we'll put on a really great show.
Himari: All right~, let's keep this up for the real thing~!
Kanon, Hina, Tae & Mashiro: Yeah~!
