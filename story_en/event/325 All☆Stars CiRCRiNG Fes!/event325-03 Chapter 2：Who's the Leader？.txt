event325-03 Chapter 2: Who's the Leader?

A group of unique personalities gathers, yet their musical chemistry seems surprisingly good...?

(Character: Ran Mitake, Ako Udagawa, Rui Yashio, Reona Nyubara, Soyo Nagasaki)

[CiRCLE - Studio]

Ran: ...Yeah. Not bad.
Ako: Right, right?! It's amazing how everything clicked so perfectly on our very first practice!
PAREO (Reona): That was such a lovely tone. Soyo-san, your bass playing was so careful and really nice♪
Soyo: Oh, no, not at all. It's all thanks to you upperclassmen leading the way♪
Rui: With this lineup, I think we can keep improving the song's overall quality.
Soyo: Yes!
Soyo: (...The sound of that violin was just overwhelming. Yashio-senpai really is incredible.)
Rui: Nagasaki-san. You've been looking at me for a while now. Was there something wrong with my performance?
Soyo: Oh, no... I was just thinking that you really are amazing, Yashio-senpai.
Ako: Oh, right! You and Rui both go to Tsukinomori, don't you, Soyo?
Soyo: Yes. We never really interacted directly, but Yashio-senpai is famous at school. She's the student council president, has the top grades, and her violin performances are incredible.
PAREO (Reona): Rui-san's violin truly is wonderful. PAREO rarely gets the chance to play along with one, so this feels so fresh and exciting♪
Rui: As long as there are no issues with the performance, that's all that matters.
Ako: It was so cool! Ako's drums and Rui's violin joined together to form a deep melody that became the power of darkness... Umm~...
Ako: Boom! Bang!! That's what it felt like!
Ran: ...Well, I got that you had fun.
Ran: I wasn't sure what to expect from a shuffle band... but yeah. I think this is going to be a good show.
Ako: Ako thinks so too! I can't wait for our upcoming practices!
PAREO (Reona): By the way, Ran-san, thank you for booking the studio. It was a huge help having you handle the meeting time and everything else.
Ran: It wasn't a big deal.
Ran: Besides... I'm the oldest one here, so I figured I should take care of that stuff.
Ako: Huh?! You're the oldest, Ran-chan?!
Ran: Yeah... Is that a problem?
Ako: But Rui and Soyo and PAREO all seem so mature! You all feel like big sisters!
Soyo: Oh, not at all~.
Rui: I'm fairly sure I told you I'm in the same year as Udagawa-san.
PAREO (Reona): Fufu. I suppose you both have a certain composure, or rather, you're very calm.
Ran: If we're talking about that, PAREO... You're so put together, it's hard to believe you're in middle school.
PAREO (Reona): I'm happy to hear you say that♪ But PAREO is still only a third-year middle school student in training♡
Soyo: Wait, middle school... ?
Ako: Right?! You were surprised too, Soyo!
Soyo: Oh, yes~. I thought she was in the same year as us, so it caught me a little off guard.
Soyo: (She's the same age as Rana-chan. She's so mature... Well, Rana-chan doesn't exactly seem like your average third-year middle schooler either, so maybe comparing them is wrong in the first place.)
Ran: Looking at us like this, we're all in slightly different schools and grades, huh?
Rui: And different bands. If not for this opportunity, I doubt we would have ever performed together.
PAREO (Reona): True~. Playing with everyone has been so inspiring, and PAREO is glad she joined.
Ako: Ako feels totally the same way!
Ako: We've only played together a few times, but the more we do, the more excited I get, and the ideas just keep coming!
Rui: Yes. This may be a one-time-only band, but I'm sure there's something to be gained precisely because of that.
Rui: We can also bring it back to our own bands. As long as I'm taking part, I intend to make this a worthwhile experience.
Soyo: (...Bring it back to our own bands, huh.)
PAREO (Reona): Fufu. You really love Morfonica, don't you, Rui-san?
Rui: ...What makes you say that?
PAREO (Reona): Because wanting to bring something back means you want to help your band, doesn't it?
PAREO (Reona): In other words, I think it's because you love it.
Rui: Improving our skills is essential if we want to keep playing as a band. Personal feelings have nothing to do with it.
PAREO (Reona): Fufu, is that so? PAREO wants to make this time meaningful too♪
Ran: Same here. If we're doing this, I wanna make it a good show.
Ran: This band's music might show us something new too...
Ako: Ran-chan...!
Ako: Me~! Ako thinks Ran-chan should be the leader of this band!
Ran: What brought this on? I'm only the oldest here... We don't even need to pick a leader in the first-
Ako: No, it has to be you, Ran-chan! You're the only one who can take in all our sounds and make them go kaboom~!
PAREO (Reona): PAREO agrees. I believe Ran-san will guide the band well.
Rui: I have no objections either. When we're unsure, someone needs to point us in the right direction.
Soyo: I agree too. We're counting on you, leader.
Ran: Huh? Wait a sec...!
Ran: ...Well, I did kinda take charge on my own at first. Fine, I'll do it.
Ako: Thanks, Ran-chan!
Ran: Then let's run through it from the top one more time. Here we go.
Soyo, Ako, PAREO & Rui: Okay!
(Black cut)
