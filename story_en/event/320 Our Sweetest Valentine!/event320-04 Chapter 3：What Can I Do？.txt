event320-04 Chapter 3: What Can I Do?

Moca pays a visit to Hazawa Coffee. The conversation turns, naturally, to Valentine's...

(Character: Moca Aoba, Tsugumi Hazawa)

[The Next Day]

Tsugumi: Welcome~! ...Oh, Moca-chan.
Moca: Hey~. I just came to warm up a bit~
Tsugumi: It is pretty cold today. Huh, that bag... Did you stop by Yamabuki Bakery?
Moca: Good eye~. I'm gonna have these buns for a snack~
Moca: Tsugu, are you busy~? I was thinking we could have tea together~
Tsugumi: Fufu, I was about to say the same thing. The shop's pretty quiet, so I'll go ask for a break.
Moca: Yay~
Tsugumi: Okaaay. Right this way, then.
(Black cut)
Tsugumi: Sorry to keep you waiting. Here's your hot milk tea. I went ahead and made the same for myself, too.
Moca: Thanks~, Tsugu.
Moca: So, how's your Valentine's prep going~? You looked like you were stressing over it pretty hard~
Tsugumi: Hmm... I still haven't found anything that makes me go, "This is it!"
Tsugumi: But I'm talking it over with my mom right now. I might be close to finding something that'll make everyone happy!
Moca: Gotcha, gotcha~. You were all Squish squish at school, so I was wondering what was up~
Tsugumi: Fufu, thanks, Moca-chan.
Tsugumi: So, Moca-chan, you already know what you're doing for Valentine's, right?
Moca: It's a secret~. Moca-chan's a mysterious beauty, you know~.
Tsugumi: I see... Then maybe I'll try thinking like a detective, just like you always do, Moca-chan?
Tsugumi: Aoba-kun, I take it the secret is hidden inside that Yamabuki Bakery bag...!
Moca: Not even close~. Tsugu is still a rookie detective~.
Tsugumi: Ugh... I thought I might be able to guess it~.
Moca: My point card finally filled up today, so I just went to trade it in for some buns~.
Tsugumi: Oh, that's what it was. What kind did you get today?
Moca: A heart-shaped strawberry melon bun~. It's only available for Valentine's season, apparently~.
Moca: Here, look~. It's a pink heart~.
Tsugumi: Wow, it's adorable! The pattern looks like quilting!
Moca: And apparently it has chocolate cream inside, too~. Saya's dad sure knows how to do surprises~.
Tsugumi: Yeah, he really does.
Tsugumi: ...Hey, Moca-chan. What kind of Valentine's would make you happy?
Moca: Huh? Let's see...
Moca: Obviously, a "super-duper special Valentine's"~?
Tsugumi: I-I mean, sure, but... that's not what I meant~...!
Moca: ...Tsugu?
Tsugumi: Come on, you said it too, didn't you? That you want it to be a deep day you'll never forget.
Tsugumi: So I was thinking... what can I do to make that happen~...!
Moca: Mm~... I think just having a party together would do it, though~...
Tsugumi's Mother: Tsugumi, Moca-chan, sorry to interrupt, but something from earlier just reminded me of something.
Tsugumi: Earlier... you mean about Valentine's?
Tsugumi's Mother: Yeah. We did a chocolate fountain at a downtown event before. I was thinking that if I talk to someone down there, we might be able to borrow the machine.
Moca & Tsugumi: A chocolate fountain?!
Moca: Wait, is that... the one where chocolate comes flowing down in this big gush, and you dip fruit and marshmallows into it~?
Tsugumi: Yeah, that! The one that's stacked up in tiers like a tower!
Moca: Ooh~~! A dream waterfall...! All you can dip, all you can eat~!
Tsugumi: Moca-chan, you wanna try it?!
Moca: Indubitably~! If you had a chocolate fountain, Tsugu's place would basically be like a chocolate theme park~!
Tsugumi: (Moca-chan's grinning ear to ear...!)
Tsugumi: Mom, I wanna borrow that! Who should I ask?!
Tsugumi's Mother: Fufu, don't rush. I'll talk to the folks downtown for you.
Tsugumi: Thanks, Mom!
Tsugumi: Moca-chan, I'm going with the chocolate fountain! I'll prep so many dippables, it'll feel like a theme park!
Moca: B-blessed Tsugu-sama~~!! I am not worthy, I am not worthy~
Moca: And while you're at it, could you maybe hear out my topping requests, too~...?
Tsugumi: Of course! Tell me anything!
Moca: I wanna eat fruit~ And marshmallows and biscuits... Oh, and cream puffs and castella seem like they'd go great with it too~...!
Tsugumi: Wow, all of that sounds so good! Anything else?
Moca: I want some salty snacks too~. They're good as-is, or you can drizzle chocolate on them for that sweet-salty vibe~
Moca: Man~, a chocolate fountain really is something...! The ideas keep pouring out like a waterfall~
Tsugumi: Fufu, then wanna come shopping with me, Moca-chan? I figured it'd be better to look around at the supermarket and decide.
Moca: Yay~! I'll tag aloooong for the shopping trip!
Tsugumi: Yup, let's go!
