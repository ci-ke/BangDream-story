event321-01 Opening: A Fight?!

On the way home, Kasumi and Arisa chat about their next plans. But a small misunderstanding creeps in--

(Character: Kasumi Toyama, Arisa Ichigaya)

[Walking Home]

[Riverside]

Kasumi: So, I was copying notes off the board with a ballpoint pen the whole time, and it got lighter and lighter until I finally couldn't write anymore~
Arisa: Don't take notes with a ballpoint pen. It's hard to write with... And if your mechanical pencil runs out of lead, just go buy some right away.
Kasumi: Ehehe... I totally forgot to buy it. Seriously, thanks so much back then, Arisa!
Arisa: You scared me, y'know. In the middle of class, you suddenly went, "Arisa~, give me some pencil lead~!" And everyone around us was snickering.
Arisa: Well, at least it woke me up, so that was nice. The period before was P.E., after all.
Kasumi: Seriously, why do you get so sleepy after P.E.?
Arisa: Well, you use your body. ...Though you doze off even when it's not P.E., don't you?
Kasumi: Huh? Do I~?
Arisa: In history class, and especially when math gets into the hard stuff.
Kasumi: Ugh... If we could eat snacks during class or something, I'd perk right up~... Like a c-cold ice cream!
Arisa: Are you a grade schooler...? Try listening to the teacher a little more seriously.
Kasumi: Oh! Speaking of which, Arisa! Did you hear the ice cream shop has new flavors?!
Arisa: The one by the station? What kind?
Kasumi: A ton of different ones! They added a bunch of new flavors!
Kasumi: I happened to walk by the other day, and there was a huge line right in front of the place~!
Arisa: In this freezing cold? It's that popular...?
Kasumi: They've got a fair going on, and get this... every flavor's half off! Half off! Isn't that awesome?!
Arisa: ...Ah, so that's why there's a line. But still, do you really want it that bad?
Kasumi: Ice cream in winter tastes amazing~! Hmm-hmm~♪ Maybe grape~... Strawberry sounds good too~...
Kasumi: All right! Then I'll just stack 'em up! How many scoops should I get~? Hey, Arisa, what about you? How many are you getting?
Arisa: Mmm... I don't really need to stack them that high. And why are we acting like we're going?
Kasumi: Come oooon~, let's go! Next Sunday!
Kasumi: That promise we made last time got canceled because of my schedule. Let's have extra fun to make up for it! Okay, Arisa!
Arisa: Sigh~... good grief... Don't drag me all over the place, okay~?
Arisa: ...Huh? Wait a second. Did you just say Sunday?
Kasumi: Yeah! Our new plan's on Sunday, right?
Arisa: No... wasn't it Saturday?
Kasumi: Huh...?! Was it...?
Arisa: It definitely was. Hold on, I'll check our messages on my phone...
Arisa: See? You wrote, plain as day, "Can't wait for Saturday~!"
Kasumi: Ah~~~~! You're right~~~!
Kasumi: Sorry, Arisa~~! I thought it was Sunday this whole time...!
Arisa: Hey, hey... Did you schedule something else?
Kasumi: Yeah... Those kids are doing their first show at RiNG that day.
Kasumi: I'm in charge of the stage production, so if I'm not there...
Arisa: ...Well, yeah, that takes priority. Make sure their first show turns out great.
Kasumi: I'm sooo~ sorry~~! Next time, I swear! I'll make it up to you!
Arisa: ...Jeez, this is the second time. You really are impossible, Kasumi.
Kasumi: Ehehe... Thanks.
Kasumi: I knew you'd forgive me, Arisa~
Arisa: ..........Hold on.
Kasumi: Huh? What's wrong?
Arisa: What's that supposed to mean? Don't tell me... You think it's fine to blow off plans if it's me?
Kasumi: N-no! That's not what I meant when I said that...!
Arisa: I dunno... It just makes me feel like an idiot.
Kasumi: Huh?
Arisa: Ghhh...! Whatever, I don't care!
Kasumi: A-Arisa~~~!
