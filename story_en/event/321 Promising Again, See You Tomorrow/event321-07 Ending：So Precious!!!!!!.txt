event321-07 Ending: So Precious!!!!!!

On the walk home, they trade see you tomorrow" again and again. The usual, everyday moments feel a little more precious.

(Character: Kasumi Toyama, Tae Hanazono, Rimi Ushigome, Saya Yamabuki, Arisa Ichigaya)

[Walking Home]

Kasumi: Phew~ We played a ton today~
Arisa: Yeah. We met up this morning, but it's already pitch-black outside.
Arisa: Today seriously flew by.
Kasumi: Hey, wanna hang out just a little longer? Let's grab something warm to drink and chat in the park!
Arisa: No way. School starts back up tomorrow. Kasumi, you haven't finished your homework at all, have you?
Kasumi: Ugh, yeah, but... How'd you know? Are you an esper...?
Arisa: Of course I'd know that much. It's been over two years since we met.
Kasumi: Oh, right. We've been together that long, huh... us.
Arisa: ...Yeah.
Arisa: L-look. If we keep talking, we'll never get home. I'm heading this way, so be careful on your way back.
Kasumi: Yeah. Arisa, no stopping anywhere on the way home, okay?!
Arisa: That's my line. All right, see you tomorrow.
Kasumi: Yeah, see you tomorrow! ...Ehehe.
Arisa: Huh? What's up?
Kasumi: It's just... I feel like it's been a while since I've gotten to say it. "See you tomorrow."
Arisa: Oh, now that you mention it... It might be the first time in a while I've actually said it out loud.
Kasumi: "See you tomorrow" is so nice. It's just a normal phrase, but it feels super special.
Arisa: Yeah... special, but not special... or something. I don't really get it myself, but...
Arisa: Haha, maybe I picked up your way of talking, Kasumi.
Kasumi: Works for me, works for me! It means we're getting through to each other!
Arisa: Well... yeah. Guess that's what matters.
Arisa: All right, it's gonna be a problem if we stay out any later, so we're really calling it a day.
Arisa: See you tomorrow, Kasumi.
Kasumi: Yeah! See you tomorrow!
Kasumi: Arisa~!
Arisa: Huh?
Kasumi: See you tomorrow~!
Arisa: Just go home already! See you tomorrow~!
Arisa: ...See you tomorrow, huh.
Arisa: Heh... It's really nice. It really is.
