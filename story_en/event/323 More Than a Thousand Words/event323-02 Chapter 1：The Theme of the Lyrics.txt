event323-02 Chapter 1: The Theme of the Lyrics

With the mini-album confirmed, the five of them waste no time meeting to discuss the lyrics.

(Character: Aya Maruyama, Hina Hikawa, Chisato Shirasagi, Maya Yamato, Eve Wakamiya)

[The Next Day]

Chisato: All right, then... Shall we get started with our mini album meeting?
Maya: Yes! First, we need to decide on a theme for the lyrics, right?
Hina: Ohhh, so you don't just start writing right away.
Chisato: Right. If we jump in immediately, it’ll end up feeling unfocused. It’s our first time, so let’s all be proactive about sharing ideas.
Aya: I mean, this is our first time writing the lyrics ourselves!
Eve: Yes, my heart is pounding!
Maya: Same here. Up until now, we’ve discussed the meaning and themes of the lyrics for songs we were given, but...
Maya: This time, we’re creating all of that ourselves from scratch.
Hina: Totally. Hmm... What should we make the theme?
Eve: There are so many things I want to do, I can’t decide.
Chisato: Yes. So I have a suggestion... How about we reread the lyrics we’ve been provided so far, and use them as reference?
Aya: That’s a great idea! I’ve never really looked at Pastel＊Palettes’s lyrics from that perspective before!
(Black cut)
Aya: Man, this takes me back... When I first got the lyrics for “Shuwarin☆Dreamin,” I got so excited just reading them, I couldn’t wait to sing it.
Chisato: Right. After all, that’s Pastel＊Palettes’s song of beginnings.
Maya: The lyrics really capture that tension and excitement of a moment when something new is about to begin.
Hina: Yeah, yeah, no matter how many times we play it, I never get sick of it~
Chisato: Eve-chan, is there a set of lyrics that especially stuck with you?
Eve: For me, it's gotta be "Takeover A to Z☆"!
Eve: It really gets me feeling brave, and those lines between verses... plus the "Pastel＊Palettes Heart, Soul, and Spirit! Bang!!!!!" chant is so much fun!
Hina: You like it so much you even made it the title of a radio segment, huh.
Chisato: Overall, it's such a playful song.
Aya: Yeah, stuff like that really sticks with you, doesn't it?
Hina: So, Chisato-chan, how about you?
Chisato: Hmm... I'd say "Hanamaru◎Andante."
Maya: Oh, is this about ViVidCanvas?
Chisato: Yes. Even with the same lyrics, the impression changes completely depending on who's singing them and the situation. That really made me realize it.
Eve: It really was an experience that made us feel just how much depth lyrics can have.
Maya: Oh, right. Speaking of that live show... "TITLE IDOL" was incredible too!
Aya: I was gonna say that too! It's like the lyrics stuck with me more than usual...
Hina: Ahh, I think I get it! It made me excited for the future, and I was totally boppidy boppin'♪
Chisato: Yes. Continuing on as idols... Our resolve and the song itself felt like they became one.
Aya: We were the ones singing, but it felt like the lyrics were giving us a push from behind.
Eve: That's right... Looking back on it, every song is a treasure that supports us.
Chisato: Yes. We've been able to sing so many songs, and each one means something special to me.
Aya: And I'm sure for our fans too, there are tons of songs that are special to them.
Hina: Hmm, then which song should we use as a reference?
Maya: That's a tough one... If we're doing this, I want our fans to be happy with it.
Chisato: True. Without our fans cheering us on in the first place, we wouldn't have been able to keep singing.
Aya: Our fans cheering us on...
Aya: Oh!
Eve: Aya-san?
Aya: Talking about it just now made me think... In that case, I want the theme of the lyrics to be "gratitude to all our fans"!
Eve: Wow, that's really wonderful!
Eve: Oh, then maybe I could put our thanks into the lyrics... for letters and radio submissions...
Chisato: I think that's lovely too, but wouldn't that overlap a bit with "gratitude to all our fans"?
Maya: Well... yeah... Hmm, what should we do...?
Hina: Does it really matter if they overlap?
Hina: 'Cause I wanna try that theme too!
Chisato: I see... So we make the album's overall theme "Gratitude to all our fans." That could work.
Aya: An album packed full of "Gratitude to all our fans"...! That's so wonderful!
Maya: I agree too!
Hina: Then it's settled♪
Eve: So that means... are we going to start writing lyrics now?
Maya: Yeah... It might be tough to suddenly finish an entire song all at once.
Chisato: Right. To make sure we go step by step, let's make it homework to think about what kind of image each of us will use to express the theme.
Hina: Okay♪
Aya: Fufu, it feels like we're finally getting started... I'm so excited!
Eve: Yes! I want to hurry and shape all these overflowing feelings into something!
Chisato: Yeah. Let's do our best so we can make all our fans happy!
All: Hoh~♪
