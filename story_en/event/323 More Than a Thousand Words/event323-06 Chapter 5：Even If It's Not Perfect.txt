event323-06 Chapter 5: Even If It's Not Perfect

Once again, Chisato confronts herself through her lyrics. And the emotions welling up within her...

(Character: Aya Maruyama, Hina Hikawa, Chisato Shirasagi, Maya Yamato, Eve Wakamiya)

[Later That Night]

Chisato: This is... "Thank you"... No, that isn't it. There has to be something more beautiful...
Chisato: .........
Chisato: It's no use. I can't just sort it out that easily.

Chisato: (I've done it all this time, and to me, that's what it meant to take my idol activities seriously.)
Chisato: (Because I did, I was able to meet precious bandmates and fans. And I even found my dream.)

Chisato: ..........Maybe I'll play a little.
