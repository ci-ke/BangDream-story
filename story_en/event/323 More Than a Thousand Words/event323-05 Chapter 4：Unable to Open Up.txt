event323-05 Chapter 4: Unable to Open Up

With her progress still stalling, Chisato works up the courage to ask Hina for advice.

(Character: Aya Maruyama, Hina Hikawa, Chisato Shirasagi, Maya Yamato, Eve Wakamiya)

[One Week Later]

Eve: Today's recording really got everyone fired up, didn't it?!
Hina: Yeah~. That shop they featured... maybe I'll go check it out with Sis next time.
Aya: Oh, by the way, how's everyone's lyric-writing coming along?
Aya: I'm making progress too, but even when a good phrase pops into my head, the syllable count doesn't fit... so I'm having a pretty hard time.
Maya: I get it. While you're making tiny adjustments, it starts drifting away from what you wanted to say...
Eve: But when the lyrics slowly start taking shape, it really hits you that they'll become a song and reach our fans, doesn't it?
Aya: Totally. And everyone's really looking forward to the mini album, too!
Aya: Look, if you search on social media for "Pastel＊Palettes mini album," there are tons of posts!
Maya: Oh, you're right.
Hina: A lot of people are definitely paying attention to the fact that we're writing the lyrics ourselves.
Aya: Hey, hey, Chisato-chan, look, look!
Chisato: Y-yes...
Chisato: (That's an incredible number... Everyone really is looking forward to it...)
Maya: Ah, we should get going to the next job soon... Aya-san, Eve-san, are you ready to head out?
Aya: Yeah!
Eve: All set!
Hina: Wow, is it that time already? You three, good luck~!
Aya: Yeah. Chisato-chan, Hina-chan, see you tomorrow!
Chisato: Yes, see you tomorrow.
Chisato: ...
Hina: All right, then... Wanna head home together?
Chisato: Huh?
Hina: Chisato-chan, you’ve had this "I have something I want to talk about" look on your face for a while now.
Chisato: ...fufu, I can’t hide anything from you, Hina-chan.
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[Café]

Hina: Hmm, what should I get~...?
Chisato: I’m fine with an iced coffee.
Hina: Huh, not tea? They’ve got a pretty good variety here.
Chisato: Ah... Sorry. I guess I was still in work mode.
Hina: What do you mean?
Chisato: Ever since I was little, at meetings, I always tried to match whatever the adults ordered... I guess it's just a habit that stuck.
Hina: Huh... So you did that too. But this isn't a meeting, so order what you want.
Chisato: Yeah... You're right.
(Black cut)
Hina: So, what did you wanna talk about?
Chisato: Actually, I've been struggling with lyrics... To be honest, I've hit a wall trying to figure it out on my own.
Hina: I see. I kinda figured you were having a rough time, but...
Chisato: Could I ask you something? Hina-chan, what kind of feelings do you write lyrics with?
Hina: What kind of feelings... I picture the fans, and I just write down whatever words come to mind.
Chisato: Then when you write what you're feeling like that... you never hesitated?
Hina: Not even a little.
Chisato: I see...
Hina: Which means you did, Chisato-chan.
Chisato: Yeah. Since I've been in showbiz for so long, I guess I've naturally picked up the habit of only showing what I should show...
Chisato: So even when I try to communicate everything I'm thinking, I can't put it into words well.
Hina: Huh?! Then what do your lyrics actually look like?
Chisato: W-well... They're kinda like a grade school essay...
Hina: Really?! I wanna see!
Chisato: No way. Something that messy... There's no way I can show anyone.
Hina: Do you really have to tie it all together nicely?
Chisato: ...I think I do. As a pro, I want to give everyone a perfect version of me.
Hina: Huh... I see.
Chisato: I'm sorry for taking over the conversation. Since you were the one who pointed out my lyrics weren't quite there, Hina-chan, I figured you might understand...
Hina: It's totally fine~! Plus, it feels like it's been a while since I heard you talk about what's bothering you, Chisato-chan.
Hina: Mm... Let's see...
Hina: Anyway, are you perfect, Chisato-chan?
Chisato: Huh?
Hina: I've never thought that even once.
Chisato: That's because we're in the same group, or something... Fans might not feel that way, right?
Hina: Oh yeah, the kid I met on that location shoot the other day said she looks up to you.
Hina: But if you showed her the parts you haven't been showing... wouldn't she like you even more?
Hina: Well, maybe they won't.
Chisato: Hina-chan...
Hina: But come on, it's true you won't know until you actually try it!
Chisato: ...Honestly. You really are that simple, Hina-chan.
