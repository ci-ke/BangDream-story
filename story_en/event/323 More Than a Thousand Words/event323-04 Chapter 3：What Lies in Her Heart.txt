event323-04 Chapter 3: What Lies in Her Heart

Hitting a wall with the lyrics, Chisato clutches at straws and makes her way to the library.

(Character: Chisato Shirasagi, Eve Wakamiya)

[The Next Day]

[Library]

Chisato: "Learning Lyric Writing from the Basics"... Looks like this is the section for that kind of thing.
Chisato: (I've tried pretty much everything I can do on my own, but...)
Chisato: (Pulling out lyrics phrase by phrase... comparing different ways of putting things...)
Chisato: (I copied down not only PasuPare's lyrics, but other artists' too.)
Chisato: (And still, it isn't coming together the way I want...)
Chisato: (I've read a few books that seemed like they might help, too... If I could find some new discovery here...)
Chisato: (...Not that I'm expecting much, even as I say it.)
Eve: Chisato-san...?
Chisato: Huh? Eve-chan?
Eve: Fancy meeting you here... What a coincidence!
Chisato: Yeah, I'm surprised too. What brings you here, Eve-chan?
Eve: I came to look for reference material for lyric writing.
Eve: I want to learn more Japanese so I can express my feelings more clearly.
Chisato: I see. I think that's a very good approach.
Eve: Thank you! Are you here for a college assignment, Chisato-san?
Chisato: No, actually, I was doing the same thing as you, Eve-chan... I was looking for material to help with writing lyrics.
Chisato: I still don't know how I should go about it... I figured I'd try to find even the tiniest hint.
Eve: I see. Did you find any good books?
Chisato: Well, there were plenty of textbooks, but... honestly, I don't know if they'll be helpful.
Chisato: Ah, sorry... That came out sounding like I was complaining.
Eve: No, not at all! I think it's only natural for you to worry, Chisato-san.
Eve: Because putting "gratitude to all our fans" into lyrics... means revealing everything that's in your heart!
Chisato: ...!
Chisato: (...So that's what it was...)
Chisato: Eve-chan... thank you.
Eve: Huh...?!
Chisato: It really helped, getting to talk to you.
Eve: N-no, I didn't do anything...!
Chisato: Yes, you did. You always help me realize what's truly important, Eve-chan.
Eve: Y-you think so...? But I'm happy if I was able to help!
Chisato: (...Thanks to her, I finally know what I really need to face...)
