event314-04 Chapter 3: Time as Friends

Rinko waits for Yukina at their meeting spot — but she can't quite seem to sit still...

(Character: Yukina Minato, Rinko Shirokane)

[The Next Day]

[Station Entrance]

Rinko: (Yukina-san... isn't here yet...?)
Yukina: Rinko. Sorry... Did I keep you waiting?
Rinko: Ah, Yukina-san... I just got here myself...
Yukina: That's good to hear.
Yukina: If I remember right, it's just the two of us today, isn't it?
Rinko: Yes... I really wish we could've all gone to see the concert together, but...
Yukina: Lisa wanted to come too, but there's nothing we can do about it.
Yukina: So... It looks like we still have some time before the concert, but do you have anything planned?
Rinko: Um... actually, there's a shop I wanted to visit... That's why I asked for this time...
Yukina: I see. Then let's go there first.
(Black cut)
(Background change)
Yukina: It really is a day off... Every store is packed.
Rinko: Yeah... But thanks to that, I found some really nice clothes...
Yukina: Fufu. I'm glad you found something you like.
Rinko: Yes... And it was a nice way to refresh, too...
Yukina: We've all been busy preparing for the showdown tour lately, after all.
Yukina: If I don't make myself take breaks like this, I end up pushing myself too hard... So it’s good timing.
Rinko: Then... I’m glad.
Rinko: Oh, um... Yukina-san... You didn’t want to buy any clothes...?
Yukina: I don’t really feel like I need anything in particular.
Rinko: I see...
Yukina: ...No, that wasn’t the point, was it.
Yukina: Lisa told me before that it’s important to enjoy things like this too... I guess I ended up putting it too bluntly.
Rinko: Fufu... I can picture that conversation perfectly...
Yukina: Still, having you invite me out like this, Rinko... it feels kind of refreshing.
Yukina: Could it be... you had a special reason?
Rinko: A reason... Um, my main goal was to go see our opposing band’s show, but...
Rinko: To be honest, I’ve wanted to... spend time together like this with you, Yukina-san...
Yukina: ...? What do you mean?
Rinko: Do you remember... when we got separated at the MV shoot location...?
Yukina: Of course. You mean when Ako and I missed the bus, right?
Rinko: I talked to Ako-chan about what happened back then...
Rinko: She said she got to talk with you about things she normally wouldn't, and that it was really fun... I was a little jealous when I heard that...
Yukina: So that's how you felt...
Yukina: If that's the case, we go to the same university. You could've just asked me anytime.
Rinko: I mean... yeah, but...
Rinko: I've never been very good at inviting people out...
Yukina: Well, I'm the same way, so I can't really talk.
Yukina: That's exactly why I think getting to talk with Ako that day was a good experience for me, too.
Rinko: Fufu... I think Ako-chan would be really happy to hear that.
Yukina: If so, that's wonderful.
Yukina: ...If it were the old me, I might've dismissed time like that as pointless.
Yukina: But now, I can see it as something important... time we need to help our band grow.
Rinko: Yukina-san...
Rinko: (That nobility I felt when we first met... It hasn't changed...)
Rinko: (But now, at the same time... I can feel a much deeper connection...)
Yukina: So, is there anywhere else you want to go?
Rinko: Oh, right...
Rinko: You came with me earlier, so... If there's anything you want to do, Yukina-san...
Yukina: I appreciate the thought, but I don't really-
Yukina: No, you're right... Then how about we go look for some snacks to bring everyone at practice?
Rinko: That sounds... really fun...
Yukina: I don't know many shops, but since we have the chance, let's take our time and look around.
Rinko: Okay...
