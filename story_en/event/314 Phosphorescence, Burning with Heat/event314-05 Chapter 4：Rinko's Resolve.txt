event314-05 Chapter 4: Rinko's Resolve

After watching their rival's show, what does Rinko take away from it all...?

(Character: Yukina Minato, Rinko Shirokane)

[After Watching the Show]

[Café]

Rinko: It was such a... wonderful show...
Yukina: Yes. It sounds a bit cliche, but... it was nothing like watching on video.
Rinko: Yes... I'm glad we got to experience it in person...
Yukina: They were incredibly skilled from the start... No, it feels presumptuous for me to judge.
Yukina: Still, I’m sure they’ve been steadily evolving.
Rinko: ...I feel the same...
Rinko: I’m sure that band, too... has kept working hard, every day, just to move forward... even one meter, even one step... even one centimeter...
Yukina: Those words... Mira said them at All Music Fes, didn’t she?
Rinko: Yes... I find myself thinking back on them a lot...
Yukina: It really makes you realize, all over again, how true they are.
Yukina: Even one meter, even one step, even one centimeter... Not just the band we saw today... but every band we’ll face from here on out is probably still evolving with that kind of resolve.
Rinko: ...Yes, that’s true...
Yukina: ...
Rinko: To share the stage with people like that... That’s what’s going to happen, isn’t it...
Rinko: When I think about it... it makes me feel even more focused...
Yukina: Rinko...
Rinko: ...Yukina-san...?
Yukina: There's something I've been wondering about.
Yukina: Are you pushing yourself too hard?
Rinko: Huh...?
Yukina: Don't misunderstand. It's not that I doubt your resolve.
Yukina: It's just... I've always had the impression you aren't the type who likes competing with others.
Yukina: So I was worried that I might be forcing you to do something unreasonable.
Rinko: Yukina-san...
Rinko: Thank you... for looking out for me.
Rinko: It's true... Even now, I can't say I'm good at showdown... And I'd be lying if I said I wasn't worried...
Rinko: And me researching our opponents like this... might be because I wanted to get rid of that anxiety...
Rinko: Even so... I still think... it's something I have to do...
Yukina: ...Isn't that painful for you?
Rinko: I... as a member of Roselia... I've decided to move forward...
Yukina: ...!
Rinko: There might be times... when it hurts...
Rinko: But even if moving ahead comes with pain... this path... is something I want to do...
Yukina: Something you want to do...
Yukina: ...That was a foolish question to ask.
Yukina: Hearing what you just said reminded me of when I rewrote our single.
(White cut)

(Memory cut-in)

(Background change)
Yukina: When I looked at all of your faces then, I knew that song wouldn't be good enough.
Yukina: I was the one who asked if you were prepared to bet everything as "challengers"... but I never expected everyone's resolve to grow this much.
Yukina: Right now, I can feel your toughness and strength... like you won't fear any challenge, and you'll even enjoy walking down a thorny path.
(White cut)

(Memory cut-out)

(Background change)
Rinko: Back then... you mean...?
Yukina: Yes. That decision, too, came with its own kind of hardship... because it meant answering everyone's resolve.
Yukina: And yet I could make it... because that was exactly what I wanted to do.
Rinko: Even if it hurts... something you want to do...
Yukina: Knowing you have the same resolve I had back then... makes me feel incredibly reassured.
Rinko: Yukina-san... Thank you so much...
Rinko: Honestly, I'm still not sure I've been able to... live up to what you're saying...
Rinko: Like making our pro debut... and getting to write collab songs...
Rinko: Through all that... there were times I felt lost... when the gap between what I should do and what I want to do got to me...
Yukina: Yes... that's true.
Rinko: But... that's not how I feel anymore.
Rinko: Even when things are hard... the way I am now... I think I can enjoy it... as a challenger...
Yukina: ...Thank you for telling me, Rinko. You've really... grown so much stronger.
