event314-03 Chapter 2: To Reach Further and Stronger

As Rinko works to tackle her own weaknesses head-on, a chance encounter with Kaoru at the university library awaits.

(Character: Kaoru Seta, Rinko Shirokane)

[Yotsuba Women's University - Hallway]

Rinko: Thank you for taking the time to meet with me...
Rinko: ...Phew...
Rinko: (I'm glad I worked up the courage to ask the professor...)
Rinko: (I was surprised to hear there's a keyboardist with classical roots in our opposing band, but...)
Rinko: (Thanks to that, I got a few hints on how to understand their playing...)
Rinko: (The book he recommended... I've still got time until my next lecture, so I should go look for it now...)
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[Yotsuba Women's University - Library]

Rinko: I think this is everything...
???: Oh... Why, if it isn't Rinko.
Rinko: Huh...?!
Kaoru: Hey. Running into you here... what a coincidence.
Rinko: Seta-san... It's been a while...
Kaoru: From the look of it, were you in the middle of searching for something?
Rinko: No, I just finished...
Rinko: Um... What about you, Seta-san...?
Kaoru: I borrowed a book that seemed like it could help with my stage performance. I finished it, so I just returned it.
Rinko: (...Stage performance...)
Rinko: Um... Seta-san, do you have any plans after this...?
Kaoru: No, nothing in particular.
Rinko: Then... if you don't mind, could we talk for a bit...?
Kaoru: ...Hmm, what a delightful invitation. In that case, shall we talk outside?
(Black cut)
(Background change)
Kaoru: I see... "Tips for Carrying Yourself Confidently on Stage," huh...
Rinko: Yes... Actually, our next showdown is with a band that's known for flashy onstage performances...
Rinko: Ah... It'll be faster if I show you a video...
Rinko: It's a live show like this...
Kaoru: Thanks. ...This is a dynamic performance, full of emotion.
Rinko: Exactly... We'll be doing a showdown with them soon, and...
Rinko: If I get compared to that kind of performance... I'm worried I'll come off as lacking...
Kaoru: Is that so? I think your playing is quite captivating too, Rinko.
Rinko: Th-thank you...
Rinko: But maybe I should... be more aware of how I present myself, too...
Kaoru: Fufu, I could tell you had something on your mind, but... so that's what you wanted to ask.
Rinko: Yes... You're always shining onstage, Seta-san...
Rinko: So if you don't mind... I'd really appreciate any advice...
Kaoru: A bother? Not at all. If anything, it's an honor that you'd turn to Kaoru Seta for help.
Rinko: I'm glad to hear that...
Rinko: Then... how did you learn to have that kind of presence...?
Kaoru: Now that's an interesting question.
Kaoru: First, there are differences between theater and a live show... but I believe, at their core, they're the same.
Kaoru: To deliver what you want to convey to the audience right in front of you. That's what matters most.
Rinko: Deliver what I want to convey...
Kaoru: Yes. That's what I keep in mind onstage... and honestly, that's all there is to it.
Rinko: ...So, in other words, that presence appears... in proportion to the strength of those feelings...?
Kaoru: Perhaps. I don't really know how strong my presence is myself, but if people watching feel that way... then I think it's proof my feelings are reaching them.
Kaoru: And... how you convey those feelings differs depending on the story, and on the music. Don't you think?
Rinko: ...If I can hold onto what I want to convey strongly enough... then I won't be compared based on how big or small my actions are...
Kaoru: It is... but that.
Kaoru: Every single movement onstage, no matter how small, always reaches the audience.
Kaoru: And that goes for you too, Rinko... and for the band you showed me earlier. Your feelings will always get through... or rather, it might be more accurate to say they can't help but get through.
Kaoru: I've felt that, standing on all kinds of stages... as part of Hello, Happy World! and as an actress.
Rinko: Seta-san...
Rinko: ...I was so focused on our opponent band... I lost sight of what really mattered...
Rinko: Truly... the sincerity you bring to the stage, Seta-san... it's something I've looked up to ever since we were in high school...
Kaoru: Fufu, thank you. But those words suit you even more.
Rinko: Huh...?
Kaoru: Back when you helped with the drama club's regular performance... you were fixing up the costumes right until the very last minute.
Kaoru: And even now, for the sake of a better performance, you keep questioning yourself without compromising.
Kaoru: Your sincerity... moves me deeply.
Rinko: Th-there's no way I deserve that kind of praise...
Kaoru: Fufu, and that modesty is one of your virtues too.
Kaoru: This conversation has been incredibly meaningful for me as well. Let's both keep shining onstage.
Rinko: Yes... I'll do my best...!
