event314-01 Opening: Undeniable Growth

Having pulled off their biggest show yet, Roselia sets their sights on a new goal...

(Character: Yukina Minato, Sayo Hikawa, Lisa Imai, Ako Udagawa, Rinko Shirokane)

[Downtown - Live Stage]

Yukina: ...And now, our final song. "Second to None"
Lisa: (The crowd's energy is incredible...! It's like the show just started!)
Sayo: (A concert on a scale like this... The intensity coming back at us is on a whole other level.)
Yukina: (Even so, there's no need to falter. We'll answer those voices with our music.)
Ako: (We'll give it our all until the very end!)
Rinko: (...I can feel everyone's resolve in the sound...)
Rinko: (Roselia's sound... noble and unwavering... Being able to layer my own sound over it makes me so happy...)
Rinko: (...My heart's racing... I want to make this sound ring out... more and more...!)

(Background change)

[After The Show]

[Dressing Room]

Lisa: Man, that was seriously the best show ever!
Ako: Yeah! The crowd was super fired up, too!
Sayo: When we first heard we'd be playing a venue this big, I was a little nervous...
Sayo: But it ended up packed, and it was a valuable experience for Roselia.
Rinko: Yes... I had a lot of fun...
Yukina: Yes. And during today's show... I could really feel our growth, too.
Lisa: Oh. Kinda rare to hear you praise us like that, Yukina.
Yukina: It's an undeniable fact. We've kept moving forward without stopping, all this time, to get our revenge at All Music Fes.
Yukina: Lisa, getting back on your feet even after facing hardships. Ako and Rinko, doubling down on your resolve after what happened with Udagawa-san.
Yukina: And that performance the other day with Sayo and Hina. All of it is becoming new strength for Roselia.
Sayo: Minato-san... Yes, I can feel it too.
Lisa: For real, like... the heat you feel up onstage? It's totally different now!
Ako: Ako thought so too! Like the flames of darkness evolved even more... and then BAAAM!
Rinko: So... everyone felt the same thing...
Yukina: That's right. But this heat has only just begun to spark.
Yukina: Once we've made it something solid we can use as our weapon, we'll finally be ready to step onto the All Music Fes stage.
Lisa: Yeah. But... nurturing that heat probably won't be easy either.
Yukina: No. To do that, I think experiences like today, where the pressure is on, will be the key.
Rinko: Experiences... under pressure...
Rinko: (Still... with us as we are now, I'm sure...)
(Black cut)
(Background change)

[The Next Day]

[Music Agency]

Harumi: Everyone, great job at yesterday's show! I was so moved I started crying... It was a pain doing my makeup this morning.
Lisa: Ahaha, sorry. But hearing you say that makes me really happy!
Harumi: So, while I'd love to take our time talking about your thoughts... I actually wanted to discuss your next schedule right away.
Yukina: Sure, I don't mind.
Harumi: Thank you. Actually, we're currently planning a Roselia showdown tour.
Ako: A showdown tour?
Harumi: Yes. We've been receiving offers for showdown since we appeared at All Music Fes, but...
Harumi: Up until now, we've prioritized album production and solo concerts, and put them on hold for the time being.
Harumi: But now that you've successfully pulled off a large-scale live show, I believe this is the most effective time to run it as a tour.
Yukina: I see... If it's a tour, does that mean we'll be doing battle shows with multiple bands?
Harumi: Yes. Nothing's finalized yet, but... here is the current list of candidates we'd be battling with.
Ako: Thanks!
Lisa: Let's see... Wait, what?! Isn't this kind of amazing?!
Rinko: Ah, this band performed at All Music Fes too...
Sayo: The other bands are all established professionals as well.
Yukina: It's a relief to know we've been recognized as worthy opponents by so many bands.
Sayo: That's true. A showdown would also let us gauge our current abilities more accurately.
Rinko: And... it sounds like it'll be a chance for us to get some good inspiration from other pro bands...
Ako: I think this is a huge opportunity!
Yukina: Yes...
Lisa: This is exactly what Yukina was talking about yesterday... Sounds like it'll be a pretty high-pressure experience.
Yukina: That's right. Harumi-san, we'd really like you to move forward with this showdown tour plan.
Harumi: I had a feeling you'd say that, everyone!
Harumi: Then I'll get started on the plan right away. I'll update you again soon, so I look forward to working with you going forward!
