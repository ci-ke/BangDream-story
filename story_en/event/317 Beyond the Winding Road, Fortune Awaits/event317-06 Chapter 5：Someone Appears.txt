event317-06 Chapter 5: Someone Appears

Kanon has gotten lost again. The group splits up to search the shrine grounds.

(Character: Tae Hanazono, Kanon Matsubara, Eve Wakamiya, Ako Udagawa, Nanami Hiromachi)

[Shrine]

Tae: I wonder where Kanon-senpai went...?
Nanami: At a glance, it doesn't look like she's around here...
Eve: F-for now, I'll give her a call!
Tae: Yeah. If we can get in touch, that'll be a relief for now.
Nanami: I just hope Kanon-senpai notices...
Eve: ...Ugh, no good. I can't get through.
Tae: I see... Hmm, this is a problem.
Ako: I hope she's okay. Maybe she got lost again...
Tae: She's not nearby, and we can't get through... In that case, let's split up and search.
Ako: Yeah, let's find her quick!
Eve: But how should we look?
Nanami: Oh, then I saw a map earlier. This way~
Nanami: Let's use this as a guide and split up to search, and if we can't find her, how about we meet back here again in ten minutes?
Eve: Understood! Then I'll check the approach to the shrine!
Tae: I'll... um, go check around where the food stalls were.
Ako: Okay, Ako's gonna check over here! See you later!
Nanami: Um, then I guess I'll... go check over by the shrine office.
Nanami: But first, I should probably take one more look around here, just in case.
Nanami: ...?! Huh, just now, that sounded like...
Nanami: Was that a voice I recognized...?
Kanon: ...It's okay, it's okay. I'm sure we'll see them soon...
Nanami: ...I knew it! Kano-chan-senpai!
(Black cut)

[Shrine - Outside the Shrine Office]

Nanami: I see... Kano-chan-senpai was talking to a lost kid~
Kanon: Yeah. She was crying like she was scared, and I couldn't just leave her alone.
Kanon: I thought I should get her to the shrine office as soon as possible, but there were so many people, I could barely move...
Kanon: It really helped that you came, Nanami-chan. Thank you.
Kanon: Looks like she managed to get in touch with her parents, too. From the sound of it, I think they'll be here to pick her up soon.
Nanami: No, no. I'm just glad it sounds like they'll make it here safely~
Nanami: But I see~ So you weren't the one who got lost, Kano-chan-senpai.
Kanon: D-did everyone think I got lost again...?!
Nanami: Yup~. Everyone's split up and searching right now~.
Kanon: Fueeeeeh?! Wh-why is it that big of a deal?!
Kanon: ...Oh. Come to think of it, I might not have told anyone anything...
Nanami: Fufu, you suddenly disappeared, so it really surprised me~.
Kanon: S-sorry. I caused trouble again...
Nanami: No, no, it's fine~! We found you right away!
Nanami: And if you hadn't spoken up, Kanon-senpai... I bet that kid would've been really worried.
Nanami: Thinking about it like that, I'm glad this is how it turned out~.
Kanon: ...Yeah. Thank you...
Nanami: All right, then... shall we go meet up with everyone?
Nanami: We could message them and wait here, but... there's a big crowd, so let's move somewhere a little more open.
Nanami: This way~. Kanon-senpai, make sure you keep up, okay?
Kanon: O-okay!
