event319-04 Chapter 3: Star Break

LAYER opens up to CHU². The answer she gets cuts deep.

(Character: Rei Wakana, Masuki Sato, Chiyu Tamade)

[CHU²'s Penthouse - Studio]

LAYER (Rei): ..........
CHU² (Chiyu): Haa~... What was that sound just now?
LAYER (Rei): CHU², you were here?
CHU² (Chiyu): If it looked like we could start practice, I figured we'd get an early start. It's just you today, right?
LAYER (Rei): Yeah. But...
CHU² (Chiyu): I know. I heard you playing earlier. Let's blow off some steam first.
LAYER (Rei): Blow off some steam...?
LAYER (Rei): Yeah... you're right. I've been on edge nonstop.
CHU² (Chiyu): What, did something get you down? It's rare to see you this obviously bothered.
LAYER (Rei): Mm... I just feel like things aren't going well.
LAYER (Rei): When I first started the band, I guess I thought being the frontman meant... just leading everyone onstage.
LAYER (Rei): But that gradually changed, and I finally felt like I'd become RAS's frontman, but...
CHU² (Chiyu): ...So what exactly do you think isn't going well?
LAYER (Rei): You already noticed, didn't you?
CHU² (Chiyu): Yeah. It's about MASKING.
LAYER (Rei): Yeah. She just... suddenly pushed me away, and she won’t even tell me why.
LAYER (Rei): It’s not affecting the way we play, so I thought, "Whatever, fine." But... I guess I can’t do that.
LAYER (Rei): All I wanted was to do everything I could for the opening act... but in the end, I just dragged out feelings I’d been keeping down again.
CHU² (Chiyu): ..........
LAYER (Rei): Do you get it, CHU²? Why Masuki ended up like this?
CHU² (Chiyu): ...Maybe. I can’t say it’s completely beyond me to imagine.
LAYER (Rei): Wait, really?! Then what is it-
CHU² (Chiyu): It’s only a guess. It’s not like I heard it directly from MASKING.
LAYER (Rei): That’s fine. I want to really think this through... for Masuki’s sake.
CHU² (Chiyu): I see...
CHU² (Chiyu): It’s probably because you grew... and became a "star."
LAYER (Rei): Huh...?
CHU² (Chiyu): A "star" shining brilliantly on stage... The one who lights our way.
CHU² (Chiyu): That’s what LAYER in RAS is now.
CHU² (Chiyu): And the more brightly "LAYER in RAS" shines, the more there will be people close by whose eyes get burned by that glare. That’s all there is to it.
LAYER (Rei): ...What do you mean? I mean, that's...
LAYER (Rei): Why Masuki...?
LAYER (Rei): Masuki's always right by my side.
CHU² (Chiyu): It's not like I think MASKING is falling way behind you.
CHU² (Chiyu): But for MASKING, I guess it felt different.
LAYER (Rei): ...
LAYER (Rei): Sorry, I just... I don't get it right now...
CHU² (Chiyu): Figured. What about practice? We can move the schedule around if you want.
LAYER (Rei): No, I'll do it. I can switch gears, so I'll be fine.
LAYER (Rei): I'm RAS's frontman.
(Black cut)

LAYER (Rei): ...
