event311-03 Chapter 2: Tell Me About Your Sound

One night, as festival preparations press on, the sisters open up about how they've been feeling.

(Character: Hina Hikawa, Sayo Hikawa)

[A Few Days Later]

Sayo: ...No. Here, it needs to get stronger little by little...
Hina: Knock, knock, Si~s!
Sayo: Hina, I already told you that defeats the whole point of knocking.
Hina: Ehehe, sorryyyy.
Hina: Were you practicing the shuffle band song? Even though we just had band practice today?
Sayo: Once I tried playing it through, I found a bunch of spots I want to tweak.
Hina: Huh? I thought your guitar was perfect, Sis.
Hina: Like, how it goes all sharp at the start, then ends with that gwaaaang~ part... I love it!
Sayo: Yes, yes. Anyway, didn't you have something you needed?
Hina: Yup! I brought you something good!
Hina: Ta-da! Cream stew-flavored potato chips!
Sayo: Those are the limited-time ones...!
Hina: I happened to find them at the supermarket~.
Hina: Let's eat them together! You were curious too, right?
Sayo: I was curious, but... still...
Hina: Come on, come on~ You wanna know what it tastes like, don’t you~?
Sayo: Ugh... I-it’s not good to eat at this hour. I already brushed my teeth, too.
Hina: Then just brush again later. Besides, chatting while eating snacks before bed is kinda fun, like those nights on school excursions, right?
Sayo: Today’s just a normal day. Let’s do it another time.
Hina: Then forget the chips for now. Let’s just chat a little~!
Sayo: We already talked plenty at college and at band practice. We’ve been together nonstop lately, too.
Hina: Yeah, but still~...
Sayo: ...Hm?
Hina: It just hit me that the festival’s coming up so fast.
Hina: We’ve been practicing for the shuffle band too, and once I realized it’s really almost here, I got all boppin'♪... And then I started wondering if you felt the same way, Sis!
Sayo: "You felt the same way, Sis"...
Hina: We only played together for the first time today, but it was pretty interesting, huh! It’s totally different from Pastel＊Palettes and Roselia.
Sayo: Of course it is. When we play with different people, our performance changes too.
Hina: Maybe that’s why? I was so surprised, like, "Whoa, Sis can make sounds like that."
Sayo: That’s my line. I didn’t expect you to play that freely with people you’ve never played with before.
Hina: It started with me, didn't it? That improv battle! It was so much fun~
Sayo: It worked out because the others were understanding, but normally, I'd be surprised if you didn't get scolded.
Hina: Ehh? But it helped us get closer, so it all worked out, right?
Hina: And besides, you were the first one to notice my improv and follow along, Sis!
Sayo: That was because I had no choice... I had to keep the song together...!
Sayo: Sigh... At this rate, I'm worried about how the real thing will go.
Hina: Ahaha, yeah... I wonder what'll happen at the real thing?
Sayo: Don't talk like it's someone else's problem... This is about you.
Hina: It's about both of us. That's why it's so exciting, not knowing how it'll turn out!
Sayo: ...You're right. I'm looking forward to it.
Hina: Hey, hey, Sis, what did you think of my sound today?
Sayo: Huh? What do you mean...?
Hina: Like, your thoughts after playing together in the same band! Anything you felt, tell me!
Sayo: And what are you going to do with that? It's not like your playing will change because of it.
Hina: Yup, I just wanna hear it in your words, Sis!
Sayo: Hina's sound... hmm...
Hina: ..........♪
Sayo: (Hina, your sound is...)
Sayo: ...No, this is the same as last time. You just "want to hear it," even if you don't need to.
Hina: Huh?
Sayo: It's late, so go to bed. Come on, back to your room.
Hina: Whaaat?! You were totally about to say something!
Sayo: You've got Pastel＊Palettes work tomorrow, right? Staying up late isn't good for you.
Hina: Now I'm even more curious, so I can't sleep~!
Sayo: This is something you talk about when it's necessary. I'm going to bed too.
Hina: ...Okaaay. Good night~
Sayo: ...Hina.
Hina: What, what?
Sayo: Let's talk about it on the day of the fes.
Hina: ...! Got it. Promise, okay?!
Sayo: Yes. Good night.
