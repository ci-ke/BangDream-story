event311-04 Chapter 3: The Piece That Doesn't Fit

Listening to Sayo play, Hina finds herself thinking back on all she and her sister have been through.

(Character: Hina Hikawa, Sayo Hikawa)

Hina: Aww, we have to wait until the festival~...
Hina: Ah... a guitar.
Hina: Fufu, you’re still up too, Sis.
Hina: (...Today’s sound feels bouncy and round.)
Hina: ...At first, this was all I could hear... in Sis’s guitar...
(White cut)

(Memory cut-in)

(Background change)
Hina: Okay, next up is Sis’s turn!
Sayo: Huh, already...?! Um... maybe this piece goes here...?
Hina: Ah~...!
Sayo: Huh, that’s not it? Hmm, then~... here?
Hina: Ugh~...!
Sayo: Ugh... Hina-chan, don’t say anything. I’ll figure it out!
Hina: Got it...! Zip it!
Sayo: Hmm... Ah, I know! Right here!
Hina: Yes, yes! Sis, you’re ama~zing! Ahahaha!
Sayo: Fufu, Hina-chan, you’re amazing too! You always know right away where to put them.
Hina: Ever since I can remember, Sis and I were always together.
Hina: We mostly wore the same clothes and had the same hairstyles. We liked pretty much all the same stuff.
Hina: We played together from morning till night every day, laughing so much... I thought we'd spend our whole lives like that.
Hina: But... when did it start?
Hina: We spent less and less time together... and before I knew it, we couldn't talk like we used to.
Hina: It was like trying to force a puzzle piece into the wrong spot... Our conversations just didn't fit.
(White cut)
(Background change)
Sayo: ...Stop it already!
Sayo: How many times do I have to tell you not to talk to me like we're friends?!
Hina: B-but, Sis...
Sayo: That too! Don't call me "Sis"...! At least at school, I want to forget you even exist...!!
(White cut)
(Background change)
Hina: The pieces stayed scattered, and we ended up going to different high schools.
Hina: I kept thinking maybe it was because I chased after her so much, or because I'd said something hurtful... but I still couldn't figure it out.
Hina: Not knowing... staying like this forever... I thought that would be lonely.
Hina: But... that night--
(White cut)

(Memory cut-in)

(Background change)
Hina: Brr, it's cold...! Should've turned the heater on.
Hina: ...Sigh...
Hina: (I couldn't talk to Sis again today. She won't even look at me...)
Hina: Huh...? What's that sound coming from Sis's room...?
Hina: That's... a guitar...?! Sis plays the guitar...?
Hina: (...So she likes music. And I don't even know what kind of music she's listening to these days.)
Hina: (I wonder what her face looks like when she plays. I wonder how she's feeling when she does.)
Hina: ...If I played too... would I understand?
Hina: (If I started playing the guitar... maybe we could be like we were back then-...)
(White cut)

(Memory cut-out)

(Background change)
Hina: ...Heehee...
Hina: I bet she's got a gentle look on her face right now...!
Hina: (Every time I hear Sis's music, it's like a puzzle piece snaps into place.)
Hina: (With my music and Sis's, the picture slowly starts to come back into view.)
Hina: I'm gonna play a little before bed.
