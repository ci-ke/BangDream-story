event311-02 Chapter 1: Being Called "Sis"

As Roselia rehearses for the festival, Sayo finds herself thinking back on all she and her sister have been through.

(Character: Yukina Minato, Sayo Hikawa, Lisa Imai, Ako Udagawa, Rinko Shirokane)

Yukina: Let's make sure we don't forget how that felt.
Lisa: Okay! And this is a good stopping point, so why don't we take a break soon?
Yukina: Right. Let's pick up from here in thirty minutes.
All (Lisa): Yes! Yeah!
Ako: Whoa~, I'm drenched...! Towel, towel!
Rinko: Fufu... Ako-chan, you were really fired up...
Ako: Yeah! 'Cause we put together the super-ultimate set list!
Lisa: We really went all out again this time. Might've spent a little too long on it, though.
Sayo: That's not a problem. As we are now, we'll have plenty of time.
Sayo: Once we have the overall shape down and share the same image, fine-tuning the details goes much faster than it used to.
Yukina: Yes. Now all we have to do is focus on the sound.
Yukina: With the time we have left, I want to eliminate even the smallest concern. If you notice anything, tell me right away.
Ako: Okay, okay! It's not exactly something I noticed, but there's something I wanna try in the last song!
Yukina: Is it the part you mentioned before? Rinko, this involves you too, so listen in.
Rinko: Yes... Um, let me get the sheet music...
Lisa: Ahaha, so I guess this is our break~?
Sayo: Well, I get where Udagawa-san and the others are coming from.
Lisa: Are you getting proper rest, Sayo? You've got the shuffle band too, so it's gotta be tough, right?
Sayo: I'll be fine. We're only performing one song with the shuffle band.
Lisa: Have you met the other members yet?
Sayo: No. I was told we'll have a rehearsal soon as a chance to meet everyone.
Sayo: We won't have much time to sync up before the show, but I plan to focus that much harder because of it.
Sayo: If I'm participating as a representative of Roselia, then I have to give the best performance possible.
Lisa: I see. Fufu, Sayo, you've gotten even more reliable lately. It's like... you haven't been wavering at all, earlier or now.
Sayo: Have I...?
Lisa: Yeah. I even thought, "Wow, I really worried for nothing."
Sayo: It wasn't for nothing. Thank you for looking out for me, Imai-san.
Lisa: No, no. I just wanted to ask, that's all.
Lisa: By the way, how are things with Hina? Have you been practicing together?
Sayo: ...We have been practicing, but...
Lisa: Huh? Did something happen?
Sayo: She keeps using the shuffle band as an excuse to talk to me nonstop.
Sayo: Even on days I don't have class, she comes all the way to campus to pick me up. And at home, she just camps out in my room all the time...
Sayo: And while she's in there, it's more chatting than practice, so... it feels like I never get a moment to myself...
Lisa: Ahaha. Hina's just happy to be with you, Sayo.
Sayo: Even so, I'd like her to be a little more considerate. Honestly, she's so loud about it... "Sis, Sis"...
Lisa: Oh...
(White cut)

(Memory cut-in)

(Background change)
Sayo: What's with all that "Sis, Sis" anyway?! She doesn't get how much of a burden it is when someone looks up to you... even though she acts like she does!!!!
(White cut)

(Memory cut-out)

(Background change)
Lisa: (Back then, just being called "Sis" made all sorts of feelings come rushing up, and she looked like she was in so much pain...)
Sayo: When I think about going to the meetup like this, I'm worried Hina will end up causing trouble for the other band members...
Lisa: (That gentle voice... Now she's just an overly worried big sister.)
Sayo: ...Imai-san?
Lisa: Ah, no. I was just thinking Hina must be really looking forward to it.
Lisa: How about you, Sayo? Are you excited to play a show with Hina?
Sayo: ...I suppose it's more like I'm preparing so I can enjoy it when the time comes.
Sayo: No matter how much we sync up beforehand, Hina's probably going to do something different during the show.
Sayo: So right now, I imagine what kind of sound Hina will play on the day... and I'm preparing, mentally and otherwise, so I can match whatever she does.
Sayo: ...Though, Hina's sound is far freer than I ever imagine, so it might all be pointless.
Lisa: Is it...? I think it means a lot.
Sayo: Huh?
Lisa: You said you're preparing so you can enjoy the show, but... it felt like you were enjoying that preparation too... Like you were enjoying the time you spend facing Hina.
Lisa: And I think that’s a big deal for you, Sayo.
Sayo: ..........
Lisa: A-and here I am, acting all high-and-mighty! Sorry, sorry.
Sayo: No... I think you're right.
Sayo: Lately, I've been thinking that my habit of overthinking isn't something I can easily fix.
Sayo: How will I feel when I share the stage with Hina? Will my relationship with her change after we step offstage...?
Sayo: I kept thinking that if I didn't sort it all out properly, my future self might regret it.
Sayo: But... you're right. Maybe it's okay to just feel excited right now.
Lisa: Yeah. I think that's best.
Sayo: Thank you. Hearing it from you, someone who's always looked out for us sisters, really helped me realize what's important.
Lisa: Ahaha, you're making it sound like a bigger deal than it is!
Lisa: Sayo. I'm really looking forward to you two playing together, too.
Sayo: Yes.
