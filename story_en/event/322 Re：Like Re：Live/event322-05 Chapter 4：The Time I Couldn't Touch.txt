event322-05 Chapter 4: The Time I Couldn't Touch

A delay in her bass's upkeep comes to light. Empty-handed on the walk, Lisa and Sayo talk about...

(Character: Sayo Hikawa, Lisa Imai)

[The Next Day]

[Music Store]

Lisa: ...Huh. Maintenance still isn't done...?
Food Cart Clerk: Yes, just the bass...! I'm so sorry, this is completely our mistake...!
Food Cart Clerk: It should be finished in another hour or two, and as soon as it's done, we'll deliver it to the studio!
Lisa: Hmm... I kinda feel bad...
Sayo: Even so, waiting here would be a waste of time. If we go to the studio, even if you can't join in, you can still give input.
Sayo: We want to make our practice as productive as possible right now.
Lisa: You're right... Okay, then we'll take you up on that. Please take care of the bass.
(Black cut)
(Background change)
Lisa: Man, but I'm glad it was my bass.
Sayo: What do you mean?
Lisa: Like, if it was your guitar, you'd get lonely without it, right?
Sayo: I-I wouldn't get lonely...
Sayo: ...Though I suppose last night, without my guitar in hand, I did feel a bit unsettled...
Sayo: Maybe because I kind of mentioned it to Hina, she just kept talking to me the whole time...
Lisa: Fufu, so that happened. Maybe Hina was trying to cheer you up in her own way.
Lisa: So? Since you played that show together, how've things been with Hina?
Sayo: Hina, huh...
Sayo: ...Nothing might have changed, after all.
Sayo: My relationship with Hina has been changing this whole time. Ever since the day we each formed our own bands...
Sayo: And at some point, things gradually settled down, and we started to learn all sorts of things about each other...
Sayo: ...That concert wasn't the catalyst for change. More like one point we reached after all that change.
Lisa: A point we reached, huh.
Sayo: To be honest, I ran through all kinds of scenarios about how I'd feel standing onstage with her.
Sayo: At first, I did have a small worry that my emotions might overwhelm me and I'd mess up my playing.
Sayo: But by the time the day came, that worry was already gone... And playing guitar beside Hina that day...
Sayo: ...It was just fun.
Lisa: ...I see.
Sayo: However, I can't agree with saying it was good that it was you, Imai-san.
Lisa: Huh?
Sayo: About the bass maintenance. The way you put it, it sounded like it wouldn't matter if your bass was late...
Sayo: You care about your bass just as much, don't you?
Lisa: ...It'd be way too convenient if I thought that way.
Lisa: Back then... at the last All Music Fes... I ended up thinking I didn't want to touch my bass.
Sayo: ...I can't say I don't understand how you feel.
Sayo: I've had similar thoughts before... even if the subject was different.
Sayo: So I won't deny how you felt, Imai-san... when you thought you didn't want to touch your bass.
Lisa: I see...
Sayo: However...
Sayo: Thinking you don't want to touch it, rather than simply not touching it... means it's still there in your mind.
Sayo: It means you care so strongly that you can't help but be aware of it.
Sayo: Just like how I, once, couldn't help but constantly be aware of Hina.
Sayo: Feeling like you don't want to touch it... that alone...
Lisa: You think so...?
Sayo: Of course, the one who defines your feelings in the end is you, Imai-san.
Sayo: But listening to you, that's how it felt to me.
Lisa: I see. Thanks, Sayo.
Lisa: And while we were talking, we're already at the agency! C'mon, let's practice!
Sayo: Yes, we should.
