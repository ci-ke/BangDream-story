event322-03 Chapter 2: The First Sound

Lisa and Ako arrive well before practice starts. The two share a talk about their "first" sounds.

(Character: Yukina Minato, Sayo Hikawa, Lisa Imai, Ako Udagawa, Rinko Shirokane)

[Studio]

Ako: Hmm-hmm~♪ Hmph~♪ Ah...!
Lisa: Oh, Ako~ Morning!
Ako: Ah, sorry, Lisa-nee! You were practicing, and I interrupted you, huh?
Lisa: Ahaha, sorry for interrupting you when you were in such a good mood. ...We're both here early, huh?
Ako: Ehehe. I was thinking about All Music Fes, and it made me wanna start drumming already!
Ako: What about you, Lisa-nee?
Lisa: Same here, I guess.
Lisa: I just wanna practice as much as I can before the audition.
Lisa: I was thinking if I can get even a little better, maybe I can play differently than I did back then.
Ako: Ohhh, I get it. That's so like you, Lisa-nee!
Ako: Oh! Then let's practice together until everyone else gets here!
Lisa: Oh, nice. Let's do it, let's do it!
(Black cut)
Ako: Phew...
Lisa: Yeah, that's feeling good.
Ako: Right, right?! Like, Ako and Lisa-nee's hidden magic powers combine, and then it's like, rrrr... BOOM!
Lisa: Ahaha, magic power, huh~
Lisa: ...Ako, you've really gotten better.
Ako: Huh...?! Y-you think so?!
Lisa: Yeah. I mean, you've always been an important part of Roselia's drums, but... lately, it feels like you've got way more punch than before.
Ako: Ehehe~
Lisa: Maybe it started back then. You know, when we declared we'd aim to be the best in the world at our live shows.
Ako: Yeah. Ever since that day, I've been totally fired up.
Ako: I'm gonna get better, get stronger... and become the best drummer in the world!!
Lisa: For sure. Since then, your playing's been getting stronger and stronger. I can really feel how determined you are to become the best, just like you said.
Lisa: But it's not just that... You look like you're having so much fun right now.
Lisa: Like you're hungry to improve, but also genuinely enjoying getting better.
Lisa: No, it's not just you. Sayo and Rinko too... with everything that's happened, we're all changing bit by bit.
Ako: What about you, Lisa-nee?
Lisa: Hmm...
Lisa: I dunno. From the start, it wasn't so much the bass itself... I just loved playing with someone else.
Lisa: Ahaha... maybe now wasn't the best time to say that.
Lisa: Sorry. Before you got here, I was playing by myself and got lost in thought.
Ako: Huh? That song isn't a Roselia one, is it?
Lisa: Oh, this? It's a song I used to play when I was little. It was in a lesson book Yukina's dad gave me, and I guess playing it alone kinda brought it back to me.
Lisa: Back then, I couldn't play this part at all, and I'd be like, "I can't do it anymore!" while I practiced.
Ako: "I can't do it anymore..." So even you had times like that, Lisa-nee.
Lisa: Of course I did. I mean, I'm not like everyone else...
Ako: Not like everyone else?
Lisa: ...Hey, Ako. Do you remember the first time you ever played drums?
Ako: Of course!
Ako: Sis started playing drums, and she'd practice at home with a drum pad.
Ako: So at first, I borrowed her sticks, and trying the pad was what got me into it!
Ako: And the first time I played actual drums was when I asked sis to take me to a studio!
Ako: The first thing I hit was the high tom, and it felt totally different from the pad, so it really surprised me!
Lisa: Fufu, I see.
Ako: What about you, Lisa-nee? What was it like when you played bass for the first time?
Lisa: Me... Honestly, I barely remember.
Lisa: The weight of it, the feel of plucking the strings... the sound... None of it.
Ako: Oh... okay...
Lisa: But I do remember the view that day.
Lisa: Yukina was there, Yukina's dad was there, and the rest of their bandmates were there...
Lisa: And when I made a sound, everyone got so happy.
Lisa: That just... made me really happy.
Ako: I see...!
Lisa: So, I guess my attachment to the bass might be a little weaker than yours.
Ako: I see... huh?
Ako: ...Hey, Lisa-nee. I think you...
Sayo: Good work. You're both already here.
Ako: Good morning, Sayo-san!
Lisa: Mornin'. I wanted to get some practice in, so I came early.
Yukina: Morning. I just ran into Rinko over there.
Rinko: Good morning... Looks like everyone's already here...
Lisa: Mornin'. Huh, Ako, weren't you about to say something earlier?
Ako: Ah, n-no! It's nothing!
Lisa: Gotcha. Then let's get started with practice for today.
