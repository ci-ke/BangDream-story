<Carried by Our Feelings>

(Character: Yukina Minato, Sayo Hikawa)

[Music Agency - Studio]

Yukina: ...Let's take a short break.
Sayo: Yes, let's.
Yukina: Sayo. Thank you for going along with this sudden request.
Sayo: No. I wanted to tighten things up for our show as well, so this actually helps.
Sayo: But... just to be safe, let me ask. It's not that you were worried about our band rehearsal, right?
Yukina: Yes, of course. This is nothing more than personal practice.
Sayo: That's what I thought. Since we went pro, we haven't done much last-minute practice like this before a show, so honestly, I was surprised you asked.
Yukina: That's true. I realize I'm acting a bit differently than usual.
Yukina: But my reason is a positive one.
Sayo: May I ask why?
Yukina: Of course. This is more of a gut feeling, but...
Yukina: Since we started moving toward getting our revenge at All Music Fes, I've been feeling our members' growth more strongly.
Yukina: And I've told you a few times that their growth has been motivating me as well.
Sayo: Yes.
Yukina: That hasn't changed since this showdown was decided.
Yukina: No... If anything, I feel like it's gotten even stronger.
Sayo: That's... about Shirokane-san, isn't it.
Yukina: Yes. The fact that her name came up right away... means you felt the same way, I take it?
Sayo: Yes. From studying our opponents to her attitude toward practice... her determination has been pushing me forward, too.
Yukina: Honestly, I was amazed by that level of intensity. But...
Yukina: Precisely because of that, I started to worry a little.
Sayo: Worry...?
Yukina: Yes. Rinko isn't exactly good at competing with others. I couldn't help wondering if she was pushing herself too hard.
Yukina: So on the day the two of us went to see a show... I asked her directly.
Sayo: And what did Shirokane-san say?
Yukina: That even if it was painful, it was what she wanted to do... She showed me her resolve, loud and clear.
Sayo: ...She's truly strong.
Yukina: Yes. I even thought that worrying about her was, in a way... disrespectful.
Yukina: Of course, I believed I was approaching this showdown tour with strong feelings of my own.
Yukina: But when I saw Rinko's resolve... it was like something deep inside me was stirred into motion.
Sayo: Minato-san...
Yukina: This isn't impatience, and it's not about comparing myself to Rinko or anything like that.
Yukina: I just felt this urge inside me... like I won't be satisfied unless I throw myself into practice like this.
Sayo: So that's why you decided to do some solo practice now...
Yukina: Yes. It might not be a good thing to throw off my usual pace...
Sayo: I don't know how to put it... but maybe this is your pace right now, Minato-san?
Yukina: My pace... right now?
Sayo: Do you remember when I came to you about being on the same stage as Hina?
Yukina: Of course I remember.
Sayo: Back then, you seriously listened to my worries, even though I didn't have an answer.
Sayo: And you even went out of your way to redo a song that was already finished... to match our resolve.
Sayo: This time, too... with how you faced Shirokane-san, I feel like you understand the members' feelings more deeply than before, and you bring that back into Roselia.
Yukina: Is that... so? If it is, that makes me happy...
Sayo: Fufu. So I think something that seems irregular at first glance... like today, is part of your pace right now, Minato-san.
Yukina: ...Sayo. If you say so, then I'm sure you're right.
Sayo: Yes. This, too, was something Minato-san... and Roselia... needed.
Yukina: Thank you, Sayo. I really am glad I invited you.
Sayo: I'm the one who should be thanking you.
Sayo: After hearing you and Shirokane-san talk, I felt like I wanted to play even more.
Yukina: Fufu, sounds like you got fired up too.
Sayo: Yes. Let's end our break here.
Yukina: All right... Then let's tighten our focus and get to it.
