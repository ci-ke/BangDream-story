<Stars That Connect>

Tomori: Um...
Maya: In other words, she's saying it was great that we could all watch it together. But really, putting this kind of feeling into words is hard, isn't it?
Maya: Actually, while we were watching the planetarium show, I was thinking I'd like to write about it in my column, but I couldn't come up with the right words.
Tomori: Column...?
Kaoru: Yes, Maya writes a column for a music magazine. And her writing is truly magnificent.
Arisa: Being able to write stuff like that is seriously amazing. I respect you more every time I read it.
Maya: Th-thank you very much...! Hearing that much praise is making me blush.
Arisa: No, I mean it. It's hard to put what you're feeling or thinking into words properly. Don't you ever feel that way too, Tomori-chan?
Tomori: U-Um...
Kaoru: Fufu, it seems my little kittens are at an age full of worries.
Kaoru: What do you think, Maya? By showing them how you put your thoughts into words today, perhaps you could be of some help to them.
Maya: Kaoru-san, that's a pretty tall order...
Maya: But I did want to sort out my own thoughts too. Would you mind hearing me out for a bit?
Arisa: Yes, of course!
Maya: Around the middle of today's program, there was a section on the twelve zodiac constellations, right? That part especially left an impression on me.
Maya: The myths behind the constellations are famous, but hearing them again made me realize how theatrical they are...
Kaoru: Yes, they've influenced all sorts of stories, after all.
Maya: Right. The drama at their core felt almost like a Shakespeare play, and it made me think stories that move people's hearts have been universal from the age of myths all the way to today.
Tomori: ...
Maya: When it comes to constellations, we usually encounter them casually through things like horoscopes, but focusing on their stories like this made me feel like they hold clues to what moves us.
Arisa: I see...
Maya: One more thing... I'm a Scorpio, so it left me feeling a little conflicted about how dangerous that story is.
Arisa: I totally get that! I'm a Scorpio too, so I felt the same way.
Arisa: It suddenly started to feel personal... So at that point, I was really leaning in.
Kaoru: Maya's columns likely resonate with so many people because they stay close to everyday life like that. Ah, how fleeting...
Maya: Thank you! Anyway, I think that's about all I have to say.
Tomori: Thank... you...
Arisa: I'm glad I got to hear your thoughts! I feel like it helped me sort out what was going on in my own head too.
Maya: It's just my opinion, but I'm happy to hear you say that!
Maya: Speaking of which, Takamatsu-san, I heard this was your first time seeing today's program as well. What did you think?
Tomori: Um...
Tomori: I thought... I was jealous...
Arisa: Jealous...?
Tomori: Being connected to other stars... without even knowing it...
Tomori: Just by... being there...
Kaoru: ...I see. In that case, aren't we the same way?
Tomori: Huh...?
Maya: Um, what do you mean by that...?
Kaoru: Through the shuffle band and experiences like today, we've formed a true connection among ourselves.
Kaoru: In other words, each of our individual lights were guided by chance to shine like a constellation.
Kaoru: It is... but that.
Tomori: ...!
Maya: Fufu, I think that's a lovely way to look at it.
Arisa: So we're kind of like a constellation too... Kasumi would be so happy to hear that-
Arisa: But really, even when we watch the same program, everyone comes away with something different.
Maya: Yes. Thanks to that, it feels like our fun memories have grown so much bigger.
Maya: Oh, that's right! There was something in the program I was curious about, so if you don't mind, would you like to talk a little longer?
