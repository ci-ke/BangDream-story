Kaoru: Ah, isn't this Maya?! To run into you in a place like this... What fate...!
Maya: It really is. If you don't mind, may I join you?
Kaoru: Of course. Please, my princess.
Maya: Thank you.
Maya: Kaoru-san... You have quite a stack of books there.
Kaoru: It's research for my next performance. I still haven't received the script, but...
Kaoru: I wanted to prepare as much as I could beforehand. So I came here in search of reference material.
Maya: Come to think of it, they announced your next performance.
Maya: It’s such a rare kind of setting for a play... I’m looking forward to it.
Kaoru: Thank you. I swear I’ll live up to those expectations.
Maya: Still, your passion for building a role hasn’t changed one bit, Kaoru-san.
Kaoru: Thank you. But I’m still far from satisfied with just this.
Kaoru: Chisato would surely dig even deeper, after all.
Maya: That’s true... Chisato-san’s preparation for acting is incredible.
Maya: Even though she must be busy, she always finds the time to really study her materials...
Kaoru: Fufu. I need to learn from Chisato more, too.
Kaoru: Facing her work and her role with sincerity, and unraveling each part of it...
Kaoru: If I do that, I’m sure I can become even more...
Maya: Huhehe, Kaoru-san, you look like you’re having so much fun talking about acting.
Kaoru: Yeah, I really am.
Kaoru: Every time I’m given a new role, I get to meet a new friend.
Maya: A friend?
Kaoru: Yes. The people within the script... What do they see when they wake up in the morning, and what do they think...
Kaoru: As I come to understand them, little by little... they start to feel like dear friends.
Kaoru: And by getting to know a role... I can also meet a new version of myself.
Kaoru: A version of me I'd never known before, brought out by that role.
Maya: I see... So through your roles, you can take a fresh look at yourself.
Kaoru: I'm living one life as Kaoru Seta... but I can experience so many more.
Kaoru: To act... To be an actress... it's truly fleeting, and wonderful...
Maya: Huhehe, I'm really glad you found acting, Kaoru-san... and that you became an actress.
Maya: Thanks to that, we get to watch such wonderful plays like this.
Kaoru: You're right. If I hadn't chosen this path... I can't even imagine where I'd be now.
Kaoru: I was surely born to walk this path.
Maya: So it's fate, then.
Kaoru: Fufu... It is... but that.
Kaoru: Fate... and this path she led me to... I want to keep walking it.
Kaoru: That's why I intend to keep acting for the rest of my life.


<Someday, at That Place, Pt. 2>

(Character: Kaoru Seta, Hagumi Kitazawa, Misaki Okusawa)

[Dressing Room]

Hagumi: Kaoru-kun!
Kaoru: Ah, Hagumi, Misaki. You came to watch.
Misaki: Well, you invited us, so of course we came. Kaoru-san, great work today.
Kaoru: Thank you. Did you enjoy the show?
Hagumi: Yeah, Kaoru-kun, you were super cool! The story was a little hard to follow, though...
Misaki: True. The relationships were pretty complicated too, huh.
Misaki: And...
Hagumi: Huh? Mii-kun, what's wrong?
Kaoru: Was there something that caught your attention?
Misaki: No, not exactly. It's just, you know, that last scene...
Hagumi: Oh, the part where Kaoru-kun leaves his hometown!
Kaoru: Yes. A fleeting scene where the hero's homeland is destroyed, and after losing everything dear to him, he sets off on a journey in search of salvation...
Kaoru: It's my favorite... a very special scene.
Misaki: Yeah, it was really good. I almost started crying too.
Misaki: But there's just something that kind of stuck with me...
Kaoru: Oh?
Hagumi: So, like... Hagu also thought it was sad~. And Kaoru-kun looked super sad at the end...
Hagumi: But I kinda felt like he was smiling a little, too.
Kaoru: Smiling...?
Misaki: Yeah, I get it. Not exactly smiling, but... like he seemed relieved somehow...
Misaki: Like he was finally freed, just a little, from all that suffering... It didn’t feel like it was only sad.
Kaoru: I see...
Misaki: Ah, but that’s just how I took it. I mean, it’s not like I really know a lot about plays or anything.
Kaoru: ...Ah, forgive me. I got lost in thought. You don't need to change your opinion, Misaki.
Kaoru: No matter how you interpret the story, that feeling is something precious, unique to you and Hagu.
Kaoru: Whether you see it as a comedy or a tragedy... that interpretation is up to you little kittens.
Kaoru: That’s why I want to know even more about what you and Hagu felt, too.
Misaki: Kaoru-san...
Kaoru: And if there’s one thing I could wish for... it would be this—If my acting left something in your hearts, then that's all I need.
Kaoru: That is an actor’s greatest happiness...!
Hagumi: Kaoru-kun, you were so cool!
Misaki: Yeah. That was... totally like Kaoru-san.
Kaoru: Fufu, thank you. I'll see this through and perform the rest of the run like Kaoru Seta.
Misaki: Yeah. Good luck with the rest of the shows.
Misaki: Alright, let's get going. Wouldn't want to stick around too long when you're tired.
Hagumi: Yeah! Bye-bye, Kaoru-kun!
Kaoru: Yeah. See you.
Kaoru: ...
Kaoru: ...So I was smiling.
Kaoru: (Even during character work and rehearsals, I never meant to...)
Kaoru: (But as I lived as 'him' onstage, something inside me may have changed.)
Kaoru: (Yes... 'he' might've been the one moving me.)
Kaoru: (If she found out my acting changed like this without me realizing, she might be exasperated with me.)
Kaoru: (Her acting is delicate and precise... like her own beauty.)
Kaoru: (...What would she think if she saw me now? Would she say I've grown, even a little...? Or...)
Kaoru: (Either way, I'm sure we'll meet again. As long as I keep walking the path of the stage, somewhere... I'm sure of it.)
Kaoru: ...I can't wait to see you again onstage. ...Chisato.
