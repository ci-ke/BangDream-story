<The Period Drama of My Dreams>

(Character: Chisato Shirasagi, Eve Wakamiya)

[Talent Agency]

Chisato: All right, shall we get started on the meeting about the period drama script?
Eve: Yes! But before that... Thank you so much for taking this on. I know you're busy, and you're not only directing, but handling the script too.
Chisato: It's a short drama, so it's not anything that elaborate. Don't worry about it.
Chisato: Still, if we're doing this, I'm going to give it my all. Eve-chan, I'd like you to share more and more of your ideas, too.
Eve: Yes! I look forward to working with you!
Chisato: First, there's something I'd like to ask... What kind of period drama do you want to do, Eve-chan?
Eve: Of course, a period drama overflowing with the spirit of bushido, like The Lone Swordsman!
Chisato: ...I see.
Eve: Is something wrong?
Chisato: Hearing that title still makes me feel embarrassed.
Chisato: You bring it up so often, Eve-chan, I've gotten pretty used to it, but...
Eve: No need to be embarrassed! Your acting was absolutely wonderful, Chisato-san!
Chisato: Thank you. I do take pride in my work, but since it was when I was a kid, I just can't help it... you know?
Chisato: You must have something like that too, right? Something from when you were little that you felt embarrassed to have brought up.
Eve: About when I was little...
Eve: My mom telling people, "You always wanted to hold hands wherever you went," is a little embarrassing.
Chisato: Oh, I think that's such a cute story. Why not share it on the radio next time?
Eve: N-no, I'd rather not! Besides, now I can go anywhere all by myself!
Chisato: That's true. I'm sure your parents feel relieved. Or do you think they might feel a little lonely?
Eve: Ugh... I wonder... Just thinking about it is kind of embarrassing.
Chisato: Fufu, sorry. Okay, back to the main topic.
Eve: Um, I've already come up with the setting for the protagonist I'm going to play.
Chisato: I see. I'd love to hear it.
Eve: Yes! Then let me explain!
Eve: The protagonist was born as the second son of a regional domain, and showed a talent for swordsmanship from an early age.
Eve: However! He gets caught up in a battle for succession, is driven from the domain on false charges, and becomes a ronin-
(Black cut)
Eve: ...Something like that. What do you think?
Chisato: Sticking to his beliefs no matter what... Yeah, I think it's good.
Chisato: A protagonist who, despite his tragic origins, lives his life valuing honor and justice... I think that's a very compelling setup.
Eve: I'm so happy to hear you say that!
Eve: It's just that I've been influenced by all the period dramas I've watched, so it's a little embarrassing...
Chisato: This is a project stuffed full of everything you love, Eve-chan, so there's no need to worry about that.
Chisato: Besides, the fact that you can come up with such detailed settings is impressive. I could really tell how much you love period dramas.
Eve: Thank you! Of course, it's true that I love period dramas, but...
Eve: I wanted to show my fans the very best I can right now, since they told me they'd love to see me in a period drama someday.
Eve: Once I thought that, I just kept thinking, "I want to do this, and that," and the ideas started popping up one after another...
Chisato: Fufu... Then I guess I need to get even more fired up as your period drama senpai.
Eve: I look forward to working with you!
Chisato: I've got the protagonist's background now. Next, let's center on the protagonist and get on the same page about how the story will unfold.
Eve: Yes! I've come up with a few directions, but... As the scriptwriter, what kind of plot do you think would suit this protagonist, Chisato-san?
Chisato: Let's see... Since this is a short drama, we shouldn't make the structure too complicated.
Chisato: Assuming each episode stands alone, a good-triumphs-over-evil story seems like it would fit the protagonist's character.
Eve: That's what I was thinking too! It's a period drama classic, right?!
Chisato: In that case, the villain's going to be important.
Eve: When you say "villain"... should it be an evil magistrate?
Chisato: Yeah. It's easy for the audience to understand, and it makes a clear picture in their minds.
Eve: In that case, what do you think of this idea?
Eve: The evil magistrate takes an interest in the teahouse owner's only daughter by chance, and tries to kidnap her by using a merchant underling.
Eve: And the hero crushes that plan in spectacular fashion!
Chisato: I like it. It's classic, but it makes the hero stand out, and the casting could be fun, too.
Chisato: Fufu, for example, I bet Hina-chan would have a blast playing a villain like that.
Eve: You're right! I can totally picture it!
Chisato: The merchant role would probably call for someone who's good at maneuvering. In that case... maybe Maya-chan would be perfect?
Eve: Yes, that's exactly who I pictured too!
Chisato: Maya-chan teaming up with Hina-chan behind the scenes... They'd make a pretty tough opponent.
Eve: I agree... But the stronger the enemy, the more the hero's justice will burn!
Eve: Also, I believe Aya-san will likely be the heroine, but I wanted to consult with you about her character, too...
Chisato: Hold on a moment. My notes are getting full...
Chisato: Okay, all set. Go on.
Eve: Yes! So, it all starts when the girl is helping out at the tea shop like she always does-
(Black cut)
